1. Location and electricity account

Only Henrico County commercial taxes are implemented. Geography and a wholesale node do not verify utility service. Confirm GS-2T eligibility, secondary metering, ordinary riders, PF ≥85%, and approved non-export parallel operation.

Account and demand history

The distribution charge retains the same parallel-service contract in all comparisons. Historical peaks are not inferred from the simulation.

2. Study dates and historical weather

Eastern time · 30-minute demand intervals · one complete billing cycle. Verified rates: September 1–October 11, 2026. Full-year billing remains unavailable.

ERA5 hourly reanalysis, with source attribution and explicit interval conversion.

3. Facility load, solar and battery

Synthetic late-operating commercial facility: 8 kW closed, 30 kW weekday daytime, 50 kW evening, 90 kW at 20:00–21:00; weekend open load 16 kW. Not measured demand or a calibrated building model.

Advanced storage settings

AC-coupled, non-export operation. Storage is a historical perfect-foresight benchmark. Configurations that can cross unsupported rider load-factor or tax regimes are rejected.

4. Optional wholesale signal

Wholesale LMP does not replace the bundled retail tariff. No default hub or node is selected. PJM imports stay private, for internal use only, and are excluded from table/ZIP downloads.
