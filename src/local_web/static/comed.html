Configure your bill

1. Customer and supply

Confirm ComEd service and tariff eligibility from the account. A city or PJM location alone does not establish retail utility service.

Delivery classes use prior 12-month demand: Small ≤100 kW; Medium >100–400; Large >400–1,000; Very Large >1,000–10,000; Extra Large >10,000. Billed demand uses 30-minute averages, weekdays 9 a.m.–6 p.m., excluding NERC holidays. This differs from the overall peak and supply eligibility.

2. Meter imports

CSV columns: timestamp,grid_import_kw. Use ISO interval-start timestamps with Z or a UTC offset. Commercial requires 15- or 30-minute data; residential also accepts hourly data. The file must cover the full selected month in America/Chicago. Values are average meter-import kW.

3. Hourly supply inputs

CSV columns: timestamp,residual_lmp_usd_per_mwh. Exactly one interval-start value per hour. Use PJM real-time residual LMP for the ComEd zone, in $/MWh. Do not use a different hub, day-ahead price or an already-adjusted retail price. Capacity is the account’s billed obligation, not this month’s maximum import.
