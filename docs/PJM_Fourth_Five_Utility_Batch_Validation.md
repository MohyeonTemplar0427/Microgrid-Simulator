UGI's five omitted months contain mid-cycle tax changes with unresolved fixed-charge allocation. December 2026 residential service also needs a revised Universal Service Program rate. UGI's November and December published values were kept out of completed historical studies.

The Naperville ordinance sets the 2025 monthly customer and energy charges at $19.26 and $0.11433/kWh for FRS, and $37.71 and $0.11475/kWh for FGS. In 2026 they become $21.52 and $0.11968/kWh, and $41.77 and $0.11933/kWh respectively. The customer charge sets the monthly minimum. A separate six-month rolling PPA is calculated monthly and applied to a subsequent bill. The August 2025 survey reports the **billed** $0.00535/kWh for both classes. The supported account confirms the bill clock, 5% municipal use tax, and the lesser of $0.0032/kWh or 5% of pretax charges for state tax. Historical commercial accounts charged a different tax treatment are outside scope. At 500 kWh the published comparison shows **$84.66 residential and $104.25 commercial**. The eight external benchmark totals and the study’s component rounding are checked separately. [City ordinance](https://library.municode.com/il/naperville/ordinances/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=1330189) · [NIMPA bill comparison, Exhibits 1, 5 and 21](https://www.nimpaonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/NIMPA-Comparative-Monthly-Electric-Bills-Final-Report-2025.pdf)

Cleveland’s educational bill reconciles to **$79.62** for 500 kWh, including $37.60 of November-ending winter energy, $39.69 of recovery and $2.33 of Ohio kWh tax. Its October 11–November 13 example and the utility website’s undated class-specific factors establish different facts. Neither gives the history needed to calculate arbitrary cycles. [CPP illustrative bill](https://www.cpp.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/CPP_UnderstandingYourBillBrochure_2024_R5_Interior_BillImage.pdf) · [CPP rate schedule](https://www.cpp.org/Billing/Rate-Schedule)

Each runnable plan keeps distribution utility, state, retail supplier and PJM metadata as separate fields. UGI uses the UGI zone, Kingsport uses AEP, and Naperville uses ComEd transmission context with IMEA upstream of the city. Cleveland's ATSI planning-zone and CPP emergency-control-area labels are distinct. Choptank records the PJM balancing authority and the Delmarva system-meter context, with no assigned zone. All five leave the wholesale pricing node unspecified. Surplus is curtailed in every runnable PV study. Verified account facts are required independently of the sample coordinates and synthetic load.
