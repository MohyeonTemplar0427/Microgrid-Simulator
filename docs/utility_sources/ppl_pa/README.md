# PPL Pennsylvania source evidence

Retrieved and reviewed October 25, 2026. `sources.json` records SHA-256 hashes
of public original files and checked-in text extracts, their selected PDF
pages, retrieval methods and ignored original-cache paths. No credentials or
private customer records were used. One Pennsylvania tax FAQ was verified
through the official web-reader text after direct retrieval returned403;
its original-file hash is explicitly null rather than inventing one. Most
sources are filed PPL tariff books
or sheets. The official January 2026 business price history corroborates all
2025 commercial smart-meter quarters and the preceding January 2026 TCJA,
DSIC, smart-meter, efficiency and storm factors.

The original 2025 January book is Tariff201, Supplements383–389. The October
book incorporates Supplements400–401. The April 2026 book incorporates414,
including the bill-rendered April10 TCJA reset. The July2026 adoption of
Tariff202 and the October storm update are separate events. A URL containing
`August` is a storage directory, not a substitute for the effective date
printed on its filed sheet. Likewise, the official June–November2025 PTC was
reposted with a July12 correction only for the LP5 transmission class; the
selected RS/GS1 factors do not change on that repost date.

The source pack supports bounded complete calendar cycles January2025 through
October2026 for fixed default-supply RS and ordinary single-phase GS1.
Published November supply prices and the much older PTC history do not extend
this executable composite coverage. RS already embeds its applicable
customer/energy riders; ordinary GS1 applies its separate riders. The
expired ACR4 table retained in Tariff202 is not an additional charge on top
of its ACR5 successor.

Source pages from larger books are restricted to relevant rules, schedules
and riders. They are historical evidence, not a claim that all those pages'
other services have been implemented. No proposed rate is substituted for
an adopted schedule. The definitive April2025 residential sheet and actual
official business histories corroborate that quarter without depending on
the proposed-filing directory name.

`independent_benchmarks.json` contains independent Decimal calculations for
two 1,000-kWh complete cycles and both customer classes. These are tariff
benchmarks, not real-customer calibration data. Billing interpretation,
eligibility, numerical rounding limits and unsupported cases are documented
in [PPL Pennsylvania](../../PPL_Pennsylvania.md).
