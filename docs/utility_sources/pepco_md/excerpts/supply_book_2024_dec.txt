MONTHLY RATE
Customers receiving Standard Offer Service will pay the Distribution Service Charge, Transmission Service
Charge and Generation Service Charge including all applicable riders. The Distribution Service Charges
are stated in the Monthly Rates for the Customer’s applicable Rate Schedule.
The Standard Offer Service Rate for each Rate Schedule within each SOS Type, including any usage
incurred under associated Riders, will include the following components:
1. The seasonally-differentiated and, if applicable, time-of-use differentiated load weighted
average of all awarded electric supply prices for specific services in each year.
2. Retail charges designed to recover, on an aggregate basis, FERC-approved transmission
charges and any other PJM charges and costs incurred by Pepco.
3. An administrative charge, consisting of incremental, uncollectible and cash working capital
cost, an administrative adjustment and a return (included in Generation rates tables below);
and;
4. Applicable taxes.

RIDER “RDM”
RIDER “RDM” – REVENUE DEFERRAL MECHANISM RIDER
This rider is applicable to Schedules “R”, “R-TM”, “GS”, “T”, “MGT-LV”, “MGT-3A”, “GT LV”, “GT 3A”,
“GT 3B”, “TM-RT”, “EV”, “OL”, “SL”, “SSL-OH”, “SSL-UG”, “SSL-OH-LED”, “SSL-UG-LED”, “TN”,
“PIV”, “R- PIV” and “R-TOU-P”.
Revenue Deferral Period (April 1, 2024 – June 30, 2024)
For the Revenue Deferral Period April 1, 2024 through June 30, 2024, the Rider “RDM” is composed
of two parts:
(1) the Rider “RDM – Base Distribution” rate component, which is calculated for each rate
schedule as the difference between the rate schedule’s base distribution rates in effect for
the period April 1, 2023 through March 31, 2024, and the Company’s proposed base
distribution rates for that rate schedule for the period April 1, 2024 through March 31, 2025
as filed in the Company’s May 16, 2023, Application for Adjustment to its Retail Rates for the
Distribution of Electric Energy in Case No. 9702.
(2) the Rider “RDM – ERR” rate component, which is calculated for each rate schedule as the
difference between the rate schedule’s Rider “ERR” rates in effect for the period April 1, 2023
through March 31, 2024, and the Company’s proposed Rider “ERR” rates for that rate
schedule for the period April 1, 2024 through March 31, 2025 as filed in the Company’s May
16, 2023, Application for Adjustment to its Retail Rates for the Distribution of Electric Energy
in Case No. 9702.
For purposes of the Rider “BSA” monthly adjustment for applicable rate schedules, the rate schedule’s
monthly Bill Stabilization Adjustment authorized revenue per customer amounts calculated for each
rate class for the period April 1, 2024 through June 30, 2024 shall be adjusted so as to equal the level
of authorized distribution revenue based on the monthly revenue per customer targets in effect for the
period April 1, 2023 through June 30, 2023.
Each Rider “RDM – Base Distribution” rate component of the Rider “RDM” rate will be included in the
“Distribution Services" section on customer bills, specifically within the corresponding Customer
Charge, Energy Charge, or Maximum Demand Charge line item.
The Rider “RDM – ERR” rate component of the Rider “RDM” rate will be bundled with the Rider “ERR”
rate on customer bills, which appears as a line item labeled “Pepco Federal Tax Credit” as stated in
Rider “ERR” and consistent with Order No. 89868, Ordering Paragraph No. 4.

Revenue Deferral Recovery Period (September 1, 2024 – March 31, 2025)
The Company will file no later than July 31, 2024, an update to Rider “RDM” that will include Rider
“RDM” rates for the Revenue Deferral Recovery Period of September 1, 2024 through March 31, 2025.
These rates will be designed for each rate schedule to recover Authorized Deferred Revenues for the
Revenue Deferral Period. The Authorized Deferred Revenues for the Revenue Deferral Period are the
actual deferred revenues for the Revenue Deferral Period as reconciled to what the Company would
have been allowed to defer based on authorized rates during the Revenue Deferral Period pursuant
to the Commission’s Final Order in Case No. 9702
Rider “RDM” will be labelled as “Pepco Revenue Deferral Recovery” on customer bills during the
Revenue Deferral Recovery Period.
