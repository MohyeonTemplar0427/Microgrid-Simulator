Electric Bill 
KWH - The unit of energy used to measure electricity, equivalent to 
the amount of electrical energy consumed by a 100-watt lamp burning 
for ten hours.
Peak Capacity Charge - This charge covers the xed costs of 
generating electricity during higher demand.
Peak Non-Capacity Charge - This charge pays for variable costs,
operations, and maintenance during higher demand.
O Peak Capacity Charge - This charge covers the xed costs of 
generating electricity during lower demand. 
O Peak Non-Capacity Charge - This charge pays for variable costs, 
operations, and maintenance during lower demand.
Power Supply Cost Recovery (PSCR) - The actual cost of the fuel and 
other sources DTE uses to produce electricity, including the cost of 
the purchase and transport of the electricity from generating plants to 
the delivery system. There is no mark-up – you pay what we pay.
Other Power Supply Volumetric Surcharges - This item includes 
Securitization charges related to the retirement of certain generating 
plants in order to support long-term savings for customers.
Service Charge - This monthly charge helps cover basic administrative 
costs, such as billing, customer service, meter reading and more. This 
charge applies even if you do not have any usage for the month.
Distribution - The distribution charge is the amount you pay per KWH 
for delivering your electricity across our wires as well as maintaining 
the grid.
LIEAF Factor - Funds collected through the Low-Income Energy 
Assistance Fund (LIEAF) surcharge, a state-mandated charge, are 
remitted directly to the state and help provide heating assistance to 
low income customers across Michigan.
Other Delivery Volumetric Surcharges - This item includes charges 
related to the delivery of power to customers. This includes the 
Energy Waste Reduction surcharge which supports energy eciency 
programs for DTE Electric customers designed to help reduce usage, 
lowering bills and the cost to build new power plants. It also includes 
the Nuclear Surcharge which covers costs associated with 
decommissioning, low-level radioactive waste disposal, site security 
and radiation protection at DTE’s Fermi 2 Nuclear Plant. It includes 
the Distribution Securitization charge related to certain costs 
associated with tree trimming on the distribution system. It also includes 
the Infrastructure Recovery Mechanism (IRM) surcharge, which supports 
certain distribution reliability investments. 
Residential Michigan Sales Tax - Michigan Sales tax on residential
electricity is 4%. This applies to all bill charges except the
LIEAF factor.

Current Charges 
Natural Gas Bill 
CCF - The unit of energy used to measure natural gas, equal to 100 
cubic feet of gas.
Customer Charge - This monthly charge helps cover basic 
administrative costs, such as billing, customer service, meter reading 
and more. This charge applies even if you do not have any usage for 
the month.
Gas Distribution Charge - This charge is for the costs to transport the 
gas you use through our pipeline system as well as keeping that 
system safe and reliable.
Gas Cost Recovery - This charge represents the actual cost DTE pays 
for the natural gas you use. There is no mark-up – you pay what 
we pay.
IRM Surcharge - This surcharge helps fund the replacement of cast￾iron service lines with newer, safer materials across the communities 
we serve.
Reservation Charge - This represents the cost of acquiring and storing 
enough natural gas to maintain a safe and reliable supply for you, even 
during the most extreme high-use weather conditions. 
Other Delivery Surcharges - This item includes charges related to the 
delivery of natural gas to you. This includes the Energy Waste 
Reduction surcharge which supports energy eciency programs for 
DTE Gas customers designed to help reduce usage, lowering bills.
Residential Michigan Sales Tax - Michigan Sales tax on residential 
natural gas is 4%. This applies to all bill charges.
Alternative Gas Supplier charges - This section only applies if you 
have chosen to purchase natural gas from an alternative gas supplier 
(AGS). You will not see a Gas Cost Recovery section if you receive 
natural gas from another supplier – you will only see this section.
