On October 15, 2025, DTE Electric filed its second annual routine true-up adjustment report 
(Report) in this docket to reflect new surcharges for the period of December 1, 2025 through 
November 30, 2026. The computation of the proposed surcharges is outlined on page 3 of 
Attachment A of the Report (attached to this order as Exhibit A). Attachment C of the Report, 
attached as Exhibit B to this order, contains the proposed new tariffs reflecting the securitization 
charges after the true-up. 
The Commission Staff (Staff) reviewed the proposed adjustments and concluded that the 
adjustments were routine, the calculations were accurate, and the proposed adjustments conformed
to the methodology approved by the Commission.
After considering DTE Electric’s filing and the Staff’s review, the Commission agrees that the 
proposed adjustments are routine, the calculations are accurate, and the proposed adjustments 
conform to the approved methodology. DTE Electric is therefore authorized to implement the 
adjustments effective for bills rendered on and after the December 2025 billing month.
THEREFORE, IT IS ORDERED that:
A. For bills rendered on and after the December 2025 billing month, DTE Electric Company 
shall implement the new securitization surcharges set forth in Exhibit B to this order.
