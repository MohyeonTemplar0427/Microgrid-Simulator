Dear Secretary Homsher: 
  
 On November 22, 2024, FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric Company (“FE PA” or the 
“Company”) filed Supplement No. 12 to FE PA’s Tariff Electric Pa. P.U.C. No. 1 with the 
Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission (“Commission”). The tariff changes contained in 
Supplement No. 12 were effective with service rendered on or after January 1, 2025, to December 
31, 2025. Tariff Suppl ement 12 reflected the Universal Service Charge  rate changes consistent 
with the provisions of FE PA’s Universal Service Cost (“USC”) Rider. 
 
In accordance with Section 53.52 of the Public Utility Code, 52 Pa. Code § 53.52, the 
Company is filing Supplement No. 26 to FE PA’s Tariff Electric Pa. P.U.C. No. 1, which bears an 
issue date of June 30, 2025. The tariff changes contained in Supplement No. 26 are proposed to be 
effective with service rendered on or after August 1, 2025.  
 
The purpose of Tariff Supplement No. 26 is to reflect Universal Service Charge rate 
changes for the Residential Customer Class served under the Universal Service Charge Rider.  As 
per the FE PA Retail Tariff language on Page 186, upon determination that the USC rates, if left 
unchanged, would result in material over or under -collection of all Universal Service Program 
Costs incurred or expected to be incurred during the current  12-month period ending December 
31, the Company may request the Commission for i nterim revisions to the USC to become 
effective thirty (30) days from the date of filing, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.   
 
Programmatic changes to the Company’s Pennsylvania Customer Assistance Program 
(“PCAP”), approved at Docket Nos. M-2022-3036532, M-2022-3036533, M-20222-3036534, and 
M-2022-3036535, have substantially increased over the forecasted expenses used in that rate 
filing.  Specifically, changing the PCAP program from a Fixed Credit to a Fixed Bill or Percent of 
Income Payment Plan (“PIPP”) has increased the amount of CAP credits individual customers are 
receiving.  When the programmatic change occurred, a forgiveness plan was offered to any in -
program PCAP arrearages, which has also increased the expenses over the forecasted amount.   FE 
PA has left the E-factor rate unchanged from the November filing.  Filing of this interim USC rate 
will help to mitigate the impact of the 2026 rate.

The USC shall be calculated in accordance with the formula set forth below: 
 
USC = [(USC/SRes) – (E/SRes)] x [1/(1-T)] 
 
Where:  
 
USC = The charge in mills per kilowatt -hour to be applied to each kilowatt -hour delivered to all 
Customers served under residential retail rate schedules under this Tariff. 
 
USC = Universal Service Program Costs, which are the estimated direct, indirect and 
administrative costs to be incurred by the Companies to provide Universal Service to 
Customers for the USC Computational Year for the following programs (“Universal 
Service Programs”): 
 
• Customer Assistance and Referral for Evaluation of Services (“CARES”) 
Program 
• Customer Assistance Program (“CAP”) 
• Fuel Fund Administration 
• Gatekeeper Program 
• WARM (formerly LIURP) Program 
• Any other replacement or Commission-mandated Universal Service 
Programs. 
 
SRes =  The Company’s projected retail kilowatt -hour sales for the projected USC billing period 
for Customers served under residential retail rate schedules under this Tariff.
