Dear Secretary Homsher: 
 Transmitted herewithin for filing with the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission 
(“Commission”) is an original of Supplement No. 23 to FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric 
Company’s (“FE PA” or the “Company”) Tariff Electric Pa. P.U.C. No. 1 bearing an issue date 
of May 1, 2025.  The tariff changes contained in Supplement No. 23 are proposed to be effective 
for service rendered on or after June 1, 2025. 
 This supplement reflects changes in rates consistent with the Commission’s Order 
entered March 25, 2021 at Docket Nos. M-2020-3020820 for the Met-Ed Rate District; M-2020-
3020821 for the Penelec Rate District; M-2020-3020822 for the Penn Power Rate District and 
M-2020-3020823 for the West Penn Rate District, which approved FE PA’s Phase IV Energy 
Efficiency and Conservation Plan for the period ending May 31, 2026. 
 In support of the proposed changes set forth in this supplement, FE PA is also filing FE 
PA Exhibit A, which provides supporting detail for the Company’s Phase IV Energy Efficiency 
and Conservation Charge (“Phase IV EE&C-C”) Rates, pursuant to the Company’s Phase IV 
Energy Efficiency and Conservation Charge Rider.
