Dear Secretary Chiavetta: 
 
 In its Joint Settlement Agreement, FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric Company (“FE PA” 
or “Company”) agreed  to refund $13,596,184, inclusive of  compounded interest, to customers 
due to certain costs from its prior distribution base rate case s that were improperly classified, 
misallocated, or lacked proper supporting documentation.  In its November 21, 2024 Order 
approving the Settlement, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission (“Commission”) ordered 
that FE PA file with the Bureau of Technical Utility Services  a list that shall detail by rate class 
how much will be refunded to each rate class as a result of the refund  within thirty days of the 
entry of the Order.1 
 
 In compliance with the Commission’s directive, the information contained in FE PA 
Attachment A shows the credit to be paid for each rate  class.  The credits will be paid over the 
January 2025 billing cycle on a bills-rendered basis and will be reflected on each bill as a separate 
line item labeled, “Refund for Non-eligible costs.”  
 
 Following the distribution of the one -time refunds, the Company will submit an  
informational filing to the Commission that reports the actual refunds distributed to customers.  
 
 
 
If you have any questions regarding the enclosed documents, please contact me.
