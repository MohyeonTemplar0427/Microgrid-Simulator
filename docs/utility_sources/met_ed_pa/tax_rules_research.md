# Met-Ed PA tax, classification and billing rules

Reviewed 2026-10-27. Scope is **FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric Company, Met-Ed rate district**, RS and existing GS-Small ordinary default PTC accounts. This evidence does not establish utility territory from coordinates, supplier enrollment, tax jurisdiction, PJM prices or interconnection approval. Sources and hashes are in `.cache/met_ed_pa/sources/tax_rules/tax_rules_records.json`; checked-in text is in `tax_rules_excerpts/`. ## Classification and conservative accepted domain

- Current tariff physical61–62 and accepted Supplement14 physical15–16: Met-Ed RS is standard single-phase service through one meter, allowing at most 2,000W nonresidential connected load. That allowance does **not** itself establish sales-tax exemption. RS demand metering is triggered by an individual transformer, consumption over10,000kWh for two consecutive months, or service entrance over600A. Measured excess over25kW attracts a charge and75% of the highest excess in the prior11months. Initial ordinary scope must independently exclude those demand/ratchet accounts; do not derive historical eligibility solely from current modeled imports.
- Current physical65–66 and accepted Supplement14 physical17–18: Met-Ed GS-S is nonresidential **without demand meters**, single delivery/transformer location, ordinarily up to2,500kVA transformer capacity. An **existing** customer's total consumption exceeding1,500kWh in **two consecutive months within the most recent12months** may end eligibility; Company estimates demand and assigns the appropriate class. It is not an unconditional monthly1500kWh tariff cap. The new-customer paragraph does not contain the Penn Power/West Penn “loads up to1500kWh” wording. Penn Power's marked GSDS500kW/HP provision is not marked for Met-Ed.
- Initial GS-S implementation may conservatively require independent Company-retained GS-S/non-demand classification, a dated prior12month qualification-quantity upper bound at most1500kWh, native modeled cycle energy at most1500kWh and imported cycle energy at most1500kWh. These are declared inactivity/coverage bounds, **not** the tariff's exact classification test or proof that “total consumption” means net imports. The source does not resolve gross/native versus NEM-net classification quantity. Keep the independent quantity basis/source and history; never reset qualification history from PV operation or infer classification from optimized imports.
- GS-S general provisions A/C apply: one-year delivery contract, early-termination provisions and Company discretion for fixed/imputed usage. Restrict to established continuous ordinary metered service with no new/terminated contract, fixed usage, special contract, transformer/metering loss adjustment, nonstandard PF or other special fees. General Rule10b(8), physical49, imposes2.5% energy adjustments for certain metering/service voltage mismatches; assume none only with ordinary matching secondary metering evidence.

## Cycles, clocks, minima and rounding

General Rule10, physical46–50 (unchanged original January2024 leaves, present in the archived May2025 book), says meters are read and bills calculated monthly on scheduled reads/billing, with enumerated alternatives. No universal25–35day rule, partial-cycle fixed-charge proration formula or exact kWh/currency rounding convention was found. [52PaCode56.2](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/secure/pacode/data/052/chapter56/s56.2.html) defines **residential** billing months26–35days, with explicit exceptions; do not extend its residential definition to nonresidential GS-S. Ordinary complete calendar cycles are the initial declared monthly scope. Reject partial/shifted/exceptional cycles until their proration rules are verified. Use actual timezone-aware intervals/DST and reject missing data; do not substitute720hours.

RS62 and GS-S66 define the monthly minimum as customer charge **plus distribution energy and applicable-rider charges**, rather than an extra customer charge added twice. NEM191 preserves minimums and applicable riders. The exact interaction of negative automatic-adjustment credits and minimums is not established. A conservative implementation may reject cases when the **pre-refund/pre-sales-tax known-current-charge subtotal**, both raw and component-cent totals, falls below the monthly customer charge. This is an explicitly narrower inactive-minimum domain, not a claim that the tariff minimum equals that test. Do not constrain the optimizer to purchase additional energy to satisfy this guard. The separate January refund is applied afterward once and is never clipped by this guard.

Respect each source's clock: PTC, STAS, DSS and explicitly service-rendered riders follow their dated service windows; TCJA and DSIC are **bills-rendered**. Require an explicit bill-date fact appropriate to a calendar-cycle study; do not silently substitute the last service interval where an account uses another billing date. Numeric component rounding is a disclosed simulator convention until provider invoice rounding is independently reconciled. No quantity rounding rule is imported from ACE/JCP&L.

## January2025 refund and complete-bill boundary

Accepted PUC filing [1858401](https://www.puc.pa.gov/pcdocs/1858401.pdf), physical1/4, and acceptance [1860899](https://www.puc.pa.gov/pcdocs/1860899.pdf), physical1–2: January2025 bills-rendered, once per account, separately identified **Refund for Non-eligible costs**. Met-Ed table says **Credit/Customers with GRT** and yields RS **−$6.53**, GS Small **−$4.18**. Neither is another district's refund. January TCJA/DSIC are zero; **STAS is +0.02%** under the superseding Second Revised182 effectiveJanuary1,2025. Apply STAS to current tariffcharges and keep the separately identified prior-cost refund outside that current-charge base; do not grossup the alreadyGRT-inclusive refund again. This source distinction does not resolve the separate historical sales-tax adjustment.

RS solelyresidential can apply the known6.53 after known current charges and remain complete within the declared ordinary scope. For GS, the precise sales-tax adjustment associated with refunding prior-year improperly allocated costs is **unverified**. Section33.2(b)(2)'s invoice-discount/rebate examples do not prove that this prior-cost refund is a current-sale discount. Do not simply tax `(current−4.18)` or declare a zero refund-tax adjustment.

Authorized narrow GS treatment: compute6% on known **current** charges, subtract known4.18 once after that line, and flag **January2025 verified-charges subtotal; prior-refund-related sales-tax adjustment excluded/unknown**. The unknown adjustment is not an invented optimization constant. Later ordinary months may be complete when all dated components are known. Any annual GS result including January must say mixed complete/subtotal and preserve the excluded adjustment; no full-year complete commercial-bill claim. This preserves known refund value without falsely proving its historical sales-tax treatment.

## DER and solar-rider treatment

- Current physical187–191 and matching archived May2025 leaves establish historical/current NEM applicability to RS/GS-Small qualified TierI/II behind-meter generation operating in parallel and offsetting onsite requirements. Residential limit50kW; ordinary nonresidential3MW. These limits are not automatic approval or a standby exemption. Written application, interconnection compliance and appropriate metering apply. First compliant netmeter replacement is Company expense; customer-caused subsequent changes/AEC metering and aggregation may cost extra.
- [52PaCode75.13(j),(k)](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/secure/pacode/data/052/chapter75/s75.13.html) requires identical default-service rate structure/components/monthly charges and forbids extra generator fees except those ordinarily applicable or authorized by rule/order. NEM187 excludes PartialService/QF/borderline/lighting/stationpower accounts. PartialServices physical224/printed223 has a Met-Ed85.89/month admin fee and backup/maintenance charges for its defined general-service generation branch. Do **not** assume that branch absent just because equipment is small or non-exporting.
- Initial DER requires separately evidenced **already-active approved ordinary NEM/interconnection covering the proposed PV/storage and charging operation**, retained underlying classification and no PartialServices/special recurring fee treatment. Preserve active date/source before cycle; no invented20day rule. Storage is not independently presumed a qualifying renewable source. Exclude standalone storage, export settlement, opening credit balances, aggregation, unusual ownership/thirdparty arrangements and unverified changed contracts. Zeroexports/zerobank is a modeled operating scope, not a substitute for approval. Import charges remain ordinary metered imports under this branch; minima/applicablefixed charges persist.
- **SPVRC Jan–May2025:** retained official June2024 book physical231 gives Met-Ed **−.001c/kWh = −$.00001/kWh**, throughMay31,2025. Physical234 limits SPV selfgeneration exclusion to **GS-Large,GP,TP**. Therefore RS/GS-Small PV and storage retain the same credit; no genericPV checkbox waiver. June2025 standalone rate0 from Supplement21. Current newlyissuedJune1,2026 DSS physical216/printed215A expressly says SPVRC recovered underDSS and lists0.00000c/kWh Met-Ed residential/commercial. Do not add a second standalone SPV price after its last explicit May31,2026 service window; preserve the DSS leaf's stale narrative amortization dates as source discrepancy.
