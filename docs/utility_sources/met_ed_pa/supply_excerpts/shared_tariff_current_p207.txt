 PRICE TO COMPARE DEFAULT SERVICE RATE RIDER (continued) 
 
T =  The Pennsylvania gross receipts tax rate in effect during the billing month expressed in 
decimal form as reflected in the Company’s base rates. 
 
Each change in the PTCDefault rates will be filed with the Commission by the later of: (a) forty-five 
(45) days prior to the effective date of the rate changes or (b) seven (7) days after the last supply auction. 
The Company shall file details in support of the revised PTCDefault rates. 
 
At the conclusion of the duration of this reconciliation rider, the Company is authorized to recover 
or refund any remaining amounts not reconciled at that time under such mechanism as approved by the 
Commission. 
 
Application of the PTCDefault rates shall be subject to annual review and audit by the Commission.
