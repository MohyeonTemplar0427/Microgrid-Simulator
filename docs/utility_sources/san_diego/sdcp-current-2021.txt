              Introduction

              San Diego Community Power (Community Power) rates are established to meet standard bill comparison targets approved by our Board of
              Directors. Any changes to Community Power’s rates are adopted at public meetings of our Board. The rates shown in this document are for electric
              generation/supply only. Please visit: www.sdge.com/rates-and-regulations/current-and-effective-tariffs for delivery rates and schedules.



              Rate Period Definitions

              Seasonal Periods

              For all rate schedules, seasons are defined as follows:
              ● Summer:  June 1 – October 31

              ● Winter:  November 1 – May 31
