Service for Residential Customers with Electric Vehicles
SDG&E provides additional options available for residential customers with electric vehicles. Schedule EV-TOU-5 is SDG&E's residential rate with a basic service fee for customers with an electric car or who use electricity to run
a natural gas home fueling pump. The car must be registered with the California Department of Motor Vehicles.



Schedule EV-TOU-5 - DOMESTIC TIME-OF-USE FOR HOUSEHOLDS WITH ELECTRIC VEHICLES provides domestic residential service for customers who own qualifying electric vehicles. Effective 8/1/2026
