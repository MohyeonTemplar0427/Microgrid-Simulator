            INTRODUCTION
            San Diego Community Power (SDCP) Rates rates are established to meet standard bill comparison targets approved by
            our Board of Directors. Any changes to SDCP’s rates are adopted at public meetings of our Board. The rates shown in
            this document are for electric generation/supply only. Please visit
            https://www.sdge.com/rates-and-regulations/current-and-effective-tariffs for delivery rates and schedules.

These rates are applicable to all customers in the 5 jurisdictions served by SDCP and do not include SDG&E transmission and delivery charges.
