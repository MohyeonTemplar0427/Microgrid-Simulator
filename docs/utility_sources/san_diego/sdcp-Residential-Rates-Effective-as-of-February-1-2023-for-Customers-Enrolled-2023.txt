         INTRODUCTION
         San Diego Community Power (SDCP) Rates rates are established to meet standard bill comparison
         targets approved by our Board of Directors. Any changes to SDCP’s rates are adopted at public
         meetings of our Board. The rates shown in this document are for electric generation/supply only.
         Please visit https://www.sdge.com/rates-and-regulations/current-and-effective-tariffs for delivery
         rates and schedules.

         RATE PERIOD DEFINITIONS
         Seasonal Periods
         For all rate schedules, seasons are defined as follows:
         ● Summer:  June 1 – October 31
         ● Winter:  November 1 – May 31
