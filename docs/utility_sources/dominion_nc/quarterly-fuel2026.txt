On June 15, 2026, pursuant to North Carolina General Statute (“N.C. Gen. Stat.”)
§ 62-133.2(d2), Virginia Electric and Power Company, d/b/a Dominion Energy North
Carolina filed its Quarterly Fuel Status Report (“Quarterly Report”) for its current billing
period, February 1, 2026, through January 31, 2027, as established by the North Carolina
Utilities Commission’s (“Commission”) Order Approving Fuel Charge Adjustment and
Requiring Filing of Proposed Customer Notice, issued on January 13, 2026, in Docket
No. E-22, Sub 753 (“2025 Fuel Order”).  The Quarterly Report filing represented results
through the first quarter of the billing period ending April 30, 2026.

As discussed in the Quarterly Report filing, actual recovered amounts for the first
quarter and an updated projection of the cumulative recovered amount at the end of the
current 12-month recovery period (February 1, 2026, through January 31, 2027) indicated
an under-recovery exceeding ten percent of the total revenue requirement, which is based
on the Commission-approved fuel factors in the 2025 Fuel Order.  Accordingly, the
Company calculated an adjustment to the fuel factors approved in the 2025 Fuel Order.
The Company presented the incremental fuel factors necessary to recover the under-
recovery over a 12-month billing period, as required by N.C. Gen. Stat. § 62-133.2(d2).
The 12-month recovery period would begin August 1, 2026, and end July 31, 2027.  The
Company also presented a new tariff, Rider B1, to complement the existing Rider A and
Rider B that have historically detailed the Company’s annual fuel factors.

Subsequent to the Company’s filing of the Quarterly Report, on June 23, 2026,
the Commission issued its Order Decreasing Regulatory Fee Effective July 1, 2026 in
Docket No. M-100, Sub 142 (“June 23 Order”).  In the June 23 Order, the Commission
decreased the regulatory fee for noncompetitive jurisdictional revenues from 0.17% to
