Rate Worksheet!E8	NOTE: The basic customer charge and kWh tiers are prorated when the number of billing days is < 26 or > 40 days. If one or more of these conditions exist, this worksheet may not accurately calculate the charges on your bill. In certain instances, when your bill date falls on the last few business days of either May or September or the first few business days of either June or October, the current reading date may be used to determine the billing month.
