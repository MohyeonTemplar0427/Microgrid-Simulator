LED CONVERSION CHARGE RIDER 
 
An LED Conversion Charge (“LEDC”) shall be applied to each Billing Unit during a billing month 
to customers served under this Tariff.  
 
Residential and Commercial rates will be calculated to the nearest one-thousandth of a cent per kWh. 
Industrial Customer Class rates will be calculated to the nearest one-hundredth of a dollar per kW NSPL. 
The LEDC rates shall be calculated separately for each Customer Class according to the provisions of this 
rider and shall be non-bypassable. 
 
For service rendered April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, the LEDC rates billed by Customer 
Class are as follows:
