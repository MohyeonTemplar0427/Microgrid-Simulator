DEFAULT SERVICE SUPPORT RIDER 
 
A Default Service Support (“DSS”) rate shall be applied to DSS Sales delivered by the Company 
to Delivery Service Customers under this rider as determined to the nearest one-thousandth of a cent per 
kWh or dollar per kW NSPL, as applicable. The DSS rate shall be billed to Customers receiving 
Delivery Service from the Company under this rider. The DSS rates shall be calculated according to the 
provisions of this rider. The DSS Rider shall be non-bypassable.

Computational Year, the DSS rates billed by Rate Schedule are as follows:
