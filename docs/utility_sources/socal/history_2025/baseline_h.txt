H. BASELINE SERVICE 
 
1. Eligibility.  Each residential customer in a Single-Family Dwelling consisting of a 
permanent residential unit is eligible for Baseline Service.  Residential customer means 
a customer who is eligible for service on a domestic service rate schedule, and 
excludes industrial, commercial, and every other category of customer.   
 
2. Applicability.  Each residential customer in a Single-Family Dwelling served on a 
domestic service rate schedule shall receive the applicable baseline quantity of 
electricity set forth in Paragraph 3, Baseline Allocations, and the applicable Baseline 
Allocation shall be determined as follows:   
 
a. Basic Allocation:   
 
The Basic Baseline Allocation shall be provided to each residential customer 
unless such customer qualifies for an All-Electric Baseline Allocation as set 
forth in Paragraph b below.  
 
b. All-Electric Allocation:   
 
An All-Electric Allocation shall be provided to each residential customer:   
 
 (1) Upon application, where the primary source of permanently-installed 
space heating is electric; or  
 
 (2) Upon application, where the only source of energy is electric; or 
 
 (3) Who was the customer of record on a domestic service account on 
January 1, 1988, and who was receiving an Electric Space Heating or 
an All-Electric allocation on such account as of December 31, 1987; or   
 
 (4) Who was the customer of record on a domestic service account on 
May 1, 1985, to January 1, 1988, and was receiving a Water Heating 
Baseline Allocation as of December 31, 1987; and who thereafter 
maintains continuous domestic service on the same account.   
 
For the purpose of an All-Electric Allocation, all-electric residential customers 
are residential customers having electric service only or whose space heating 
is provided by electricity, or both.   
 
In addition to the above allocations, each qualifying residential customer shall 
receive the Medical Baseline Allocation as set forth in Paragraph 4 herein.

4. Medical Baseline Allocation.  Upon application to SCE, the account of each eligible 
residential Customer will be provided a year-round Medical Discount or Medical 
Baseline Allocation in addition to the applicable Baseline Allocation set forth in 
Paragraph 3 above.   
 
The standard Medical Baseline Allocation will be 16.5 kWh per day.  Only one standard 
Medical Baseline Allocation will be allowed per Single-Family Dwelling.  However, 
under the Hardship Clause set forth in Paragraph d. below, a higher Medical Baseline 
Allocation may be provided.   
 
Customers receiving a Medical Baseline Allocation or Medical Discount will be exempt 
from the Wildfire Fund Non-Bypassable Charge.  
 
Pursuant to Decision 22-08-001, a medical discount of 11 percent will be provided to 
Customers served under Schedule TOU-D Option PRIME regardless of the number of 
Medical Baseline Allocations. 
 
a. Eligibility:   
 
For an account to be eligible for the standard Medical Baseline Allocation, the 
residential Customer will provide certification as set forth in Paragraph e. below 
to SCE that:   
 
(1) A full-time resident of the household is being treated for a life-
threatening illness or has a compromised immune system for whom 
the additional energy, heating, or cooling allowance is medically 
necessary to sustain the life of the person or prevent deterioration of 
their medical condition; and/or  
(2) A full-time resident of the household depends upon life-support 
devices, and/or 
(3) A full-time resident of the household is a paraplegic, hemiplegic, 
quadriplegic, multiple sclerosis, or scleroderma patient.   
 
b. Life-Support Devices:   
 
The account of each eligible residential Customer will be provided a standard 
Medical Baseline Allocation following certification acceptable to SCE that a full-
time resident of the Customer's household requires the regular use in the 
Customer's home, of one or more life-support devices.   
 
Life-support devices means those devices or equipment which utilize 
mechanical or artificial means to sustain, restore, or supplant a vital function, or 
mechanical equipment which is relied upon for mobility both within and outside 
of buildings.  Life-support devices or equipment include but not limited to, the 
following:  
 
Aerosol Tent    Electrostatic Nebulizer  
Compressor    Electric Nerve Stimulator  
Iron Lung    Ultrasonic Nebulizer 
Pressure Pad    Motorized Wheel Chair 
Pressure Pump    Hemodialysis Machine 
IPPB Machine    Kidney Dialysis Machine 
Suction Machine    Respirator (all types)

4. Medical Baseline Allocation.  (Continued) 
 
c. Standard Medical Baseline Allocation:   
 
The account of each eligible residential customer, who provides certification 
that a full-time resident of the household utilizes a life-support device, will be 
provided a standard Medical Baseline Allocation. Similarly, the account of each 
eligible residential customer, who provides certification that a full-time resident 
of the household is a paraplegic, hemiplegic, quadriplegic, multiple sclerosis, or 
scleroderma patient will be provided a standard Medical Baseline Allocation in 
consideration of special heating and/or cooling needs, where the primary 
source of energy for the customer's heating or air conditioning equipment is 
operated by electricity supplied by SCE.   
 
A standard Medical Baseline Allocation is also available if the patient has a 
compromised immune system, life threatening illness, or any other condition for 
which additional heating or cooling is medically necessary to sustain the 
patient’s life or prevent deterioration of the patient’s medical condition.  
 
d. Hardship Clause:   
 
If the customer believes that the life-support device and/or a patient's space 
conditioning equipment (as set forth in Paragraph b. and c. above) requires 
more than 16.5 kWh per day to operate, the customer may apply for a higher 
allocation than the standard Medical Baseline Allocation.  Upon receipt of such 
application, SCE shall make a determination if any additional daily baseline 
quantity is required to operate the device or equipment based on the nameplate 
rating and operating hours.  The daily amount of the Medical Baseline 
Allocation shall be increased to the number of kWh so determined, and 
rounded to the next higher 16.5 kWh per day.   
 
e. Certification:   
 
SCE may require the following Certification:   
 
(1) The Customer shall have a licensed medical doctor, doctor of 
osteopathy, physician assistant, or nurse practitioner provide SCE with 
a certification letter acceptable to SCE.  The letter shall describe in 
detail the type of life-support device(s) regularly required by the patient 
and the utilization requirements, and/or certify that the full-time resident 
is a paraplegic, hemiplegic, quadriplegic, multiple sclerosis, or 
scleroderma patient; and/or certify that the full-time resident is being 
treated for a life-threatening illness or has a compromised immune 
system for which an additional energy, heating or cooling allowance is 
medically necessary to sustain the life of the person or prevent 
deterioration of the person’s medical condition; 
 
(2) County, State, or Federal agencies, using an established notification 
letter to electric utilities, shall provide SCE with information relative to a 
patient who regularly requires the use of a life-support device in the 
customer’s residence.

4. Medical Baseline Allocation.  (Continued) 
 
e. Certification:  (Continued) 
 
Within 15 days after acceptance of the above certification, SCE will provide 
a Medical Baseline Allocation on the customer's account.  SCE may require 
a new or renewed application and/or certification when needed, in the 
opinion of SCE.  Customers certified as having a permanent disability, as 
designated in a qualified licensed medical provider’s certification, will need 
to self re-certify their eligibility every four years, in lieu of obtaining a new 
medical provider’s signature or authorization.  Those customers not having 
a permanent disability will need a qualified licensed medical provider’s 
certification every two years after the initial certification.  
 
5. Verification.  The number of Single-Family Dwellings on the premises and the existence 
of the specified equipment or device(s) required to obtain Baseline Allocations of 
electricity, pursuant to SCE’s tariffs, are subject to verification by SCE.   
 
In the event SCE ascertains that the customer is not eligible for a Baseline Allocation, 
such customer may be rebilled accordingly.  
 
6. Termination of Use.  Customers shall notify SCE of termination of use of equipment or 
devices set forth above.   
 
7. Billing.  The Energy Charge shall be billed at the rates designated as applicable to 
Baseline Service up to the total of the applicable baseline quantities as determined in 
accordance with Part H, Paragraphs 2, 3, and 4 above.  Usage in excess of the total 
applicable baseline quantities shall be billed at the rates designated as applicable to 
Nonbaseline Service.   
 
8. Disconnection Notification.  Customers actively enrolled in the Medical Baseline 
Allocation shall receive an in-person visit from a SCE representative prior to 
disconnection for nonpayment.  Such visits should take place within 48 hours, or at the 
time, of disconnection.  Consistent with Rule 8.A, the representative must be able to 
assist the customer by collecting a payment or helping make arrangements for payment 
during the in-person visit prior to disconnection. Customers enrolled in the Medical 
Baseline Allocation shall not be disconnected for nonpayment if the accountholder 
agrees to enter a payment plan for the outstanding charges. SCE shall not require any 
Customer who receives a field visit pursuant to this paragraph to pay a fee associated 
with that field visit.
