# SCE solar account vintage and service-date audit

Verified September 27, 2026. An **account vintage** establishes the customer's solar program and export-credit track; a **service date** selects that day's import charges and export prices. The current engine bills verified service dates **January 1, 2025–September 17, 2026** on bundled residential TOU-D-PRIME. It does not reconstruct an electric bill served in 2021–2024. The [SCE 2025 historical tariff archive](https://www.sce.com/regulatory/regulatory-information/tariff-books/historical-rates/historical-rate-schedules-for-2025) and [2026 archive](https://www.sce.com/regulatory/regulatory-information/tariff-books/historical-rates/historical-rate-schedules-for-2026) supply the five 2025 and two 2026 PRIME rate versions, with the June 25 component replacement. See `history_2025/` and `history_2026/` for filed sheet and cancellation evidence.

NBT did not have 2021 or 2022 vintages. SCE's [export-pricing guide and Pacific-time workbook](https://www.sce.com/customer-service-center/help-center/solar/solar-billing-plan/understanding-export-pricing) distinguish the NBT23–NBT26 tracks. The downloaded workbook SHA-256 is `c88229eccf67e78f5e86c9ad4e1a8de7f946e585f49b39139621e70b55e211c7`; generated runtime data for NBT23–25 are in `src/billing/sce_nbt_historical_data.py`, and NBT26 in `src/billing/sce_nbt_2026_data.py`. Workbook Hour 1 corresponds to local interval-start hour 0, independently checked against SCE's NBT26 MIDAS file. The workbook supplies delivery and bundled SCE generation EEC separately. No CCA generation value is substituted.

For NBT, an account must confirm request/PTO year, original-customer non-equity bonus eligibility, bidirectional metering, and either a first cycle with zero opening credits or all three opening credit balances and a future relevant-period end. EECs offset eligible delivery or generation energy only; the bonus may offset remaining tariff charges. Balances at cycle end are *reported*, not automatically taken through an annual Energy Export Credit Adjustment or true-up. NBT battery export and paired-storage caps remain outside scope.

For legacy NEM, the account must confirm the NEM schedule, request/PTO dates, continuing 20-year eligibility, monthly billing election, next annual true-up date, and opening energy-credit balance. The study cycle must remain within one filed PRIME rate version and season. Annual billing, net-surplus compensation, cross-year carryover, aggregation, CCA generation and paired storage are not calculated. The [NEM-ST tariff](https://www.sce.com/sites/default/files/custom-files/PDF_Files/ELECTRIC_SCHEDULES_NEM-ST.pdf) explicitly protects interval NBCs and states that residential NBCs count in the pre-base-charge minimum. SCE's [Base Services Charge guidance](https://www.sce.com/save-money/rates-financing/residential-rate-plans/bsc) confirms the November 2025 switch and that NEM energy credits do not offset the base charge. Local utility tax and state surcharge are study conventions requiring bill reconciliation.

These account and service limits are enforced by the backend, so selecting a program in the browser cannot silently backdate a newer tariff or reuse NBT avoided-cost prices for legacy NEM. Additional 2021–24 historical service bills require those years' complete import tariff versions and account-specific solar settlement evidence before coverage is extended.
