What is the Legislative Energy Relief Refund?
The Legislative Energy Relief Fund was created as part of the Next Generation Energy Act that was passed in April 2025. The fund provides $200 million in refunds to electric utility customers in the state of Maryland to help lessen the impact of higher bills due to rising electric supply prices.
How is the Legislative Energy Relief Refund funded?
The credits are funded through the Maryland Strategic Energy Investment Fund, which is funded through payments made by utilities and electricity suppliers under Maryland’s renewable energy laws. The funds will not be recovered from customers.
How do the credits work?
Eligible customers will automatically receive the credits on their bills – there is no need to sign up or apply. Eligible customers received the first credit in September 2025 and will receive the second credit in February 2026.
The February 2026 refunds are based on an individual customer’s average electricity usage over a 12-month period from September 2024 through August 2025. Lower usage customers will receive lower credits, and higher usage customers will receive higher credits.

Where can I find the credit on my bill?
The credit will be listed as
Legislative Energy Relief Refund
on your bill.
Who can receive the credit?
Residential customers must meet the following criteria to receive the winter 2026 credit:
Have an active electric account as of November 1, 2025
Have recorded electricity usage between September 1, 2024, and August 31, 2025
Be an active account holder during the month when the credit is applied
What if I want to decline the bill credit?
If you do not wish to keep the credit, you are encouraged to make a monetary donation to a program that offers energy assistance.
