The Company is also submitting additional documentation in response to a request by the 
Office of People’s Counsel (“OPC”).  As the Commission may recall, at last year’s surcharge 
administrative meeting on December 20, 2023, OPC requested that Potomac Edison be required to 
undertake an exercise to evaluate alternative amortization methodologies, specifically those that 
Washington Gas Light Company (“WGL”), Potomac Electric Power Company (“Pepco”), and 
Delmarva Power & Light Company (“Delmarva”) utilize.  Potomac Edison agreed to perform the 
analysis and discuss the outcome and potential changes with OPC.  As promised, the Company 
undertook the time -consuming evaluation and analysis and completed the two additional rate 
designs (Alternative 1 utilizing the method of WGL, and Alternative 2 utilizing the method of 
Pepco and Delmarva) and provided estimated rates and workpapers to OPC on July 23, 2024 along 
with a request to meet with OPC to discuss the calculations and attempt to reach a mutually 
agreeable resolution prior to the deadline for this filing.  Unfortunately, after several follow -up 
requests, OPC was unable to meet with the Company.  As a result, the Company is making this 
filing and providing the calculations and associated workpapers for the 2025 EmPOWER MD 
Surcharge at this time. The Company is providing workpapers for all three amortization 
methodologies for the sole purpose of compliance with OPC’s request.  Should the Commission 
prefer one of the alternative methods, Potomac Edison is attaching clean and red -lined versions of 
its tariff Page 5-5 reflective of these calculations. 
 
The Company is requesting that its current method (“Potomac Edison Method”) of rate design 
be approved. Not only does this result in the lowest  rate for Potomac Edison’s customers 
($10.09/month, versus $10.99 with Alternative 1 and $10.39 for Alternative 2), but this is also the 
final year of amortization before going to full expense in 2026.  T herefore, any change in an 
amortization calculation would only be in effect for the amortization of 33 percent of 2025 costs , 
while the Company has been using the Commission -approved Potomac Edison Method since the 
inception of EmPOWER.
