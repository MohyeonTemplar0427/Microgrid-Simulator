Notes:
(1) Residential SOS is available for all residential customers taking service under Rate Schedule R.
(2) Type I SOS is available for all non-residential customers taking service under Rate Schedules OL, AL, MSL, SL, EMU, MU and special lighting contracts for Hagerstown and Frederick; and for non-residential customers taking service under 
Rate Schedules C, G, C-A, and CSH that during the prior year do not have a metered 30-minute billing demand that equals or exceeds 25 kW; energy consumption in excess of 6,000 kWh in any two consecutive non-summer billing months; or a 
monthly energy consumption that exceeds 7,500 kWh for a single summer billing month.
(3) Type II SOS is available for non-residential customers taking service under Rate Schedules C, G, C-A, and CSH that are not eligible for Type I SOS, and for non-residential customers taking service under Rate Schedule PH with a PJM 
capacity peak load contribution less than 600 kilowatts as of June 1st of each year.
(4) Hourly-Priced LCS includes non-residential customers who are not eligible for Type I SOS or Type II SOS, and are taking Default Electricity Supply Service from the Company under the Hourly-Priced LCS Rider.

Energy Cost Adjustment effective on the first of each month on a service rendered basis, and applicable only to non-shopping customers receiving Residential SOS, Type I SOS, Type II SOS or Hourly-Priced LCS.  This 
charge does not apply to customers obtaining Competitive Power Supply.
Administrative Credit effective on the first of each month on a service rendered basis, and applicable to all customers (regardless of shopping status) eligible for Residential SOS, Type I SOS and Type II SOS.
