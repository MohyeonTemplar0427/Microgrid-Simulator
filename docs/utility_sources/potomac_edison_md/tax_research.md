# Potomac Edison Maryland tax, eligibility and billing evidence

Research 2026-10-26. Own scope: Maryland Schedule R and G Type I SOS,
ordinary service and explicitly approved PV/storage with zero exports. These
rules and prices are independently Potomac Edison evidence; Pepco, Delmarva,
BGE and Potomac Edison West Virginia are not substitutes. No production or Git
index changes were made. Originals/hashes and selected excerpts are retained
under `.cache/potomac_edison_md/sources/tax/`, manifest `tax_records.json`.

Weather point 39.60,-77.73 establishes neither territory nor tax jurisdiction.

status facts. Hagerstown/other municipal utilities cannot be inferred to be
Potomac Edison merely from county/location.

special agreements/exemptions and unverified local surcharges are unsupported.

## USP, franchise, GRT and MES

Current 51/5, First Revision, issued May1,2019 effective **bills rendered June1,
2019**: R **$0.32/month**. Commercial bands depend on prior calendar year's
**distribution** bill amount. Selected G $175–$1,299 band is **$1.84/month**,
not the $1.85 used by other Maryland utilities. Under $175 is $0.25;
$1,300–$2,599 is $6.14. Full table is preserved in the selected excerpt.
The PE sheet does **not** state a first-quarter reassignment date. Do not copy
that clock from another utility. Require utility-assigned whole-period/year
band evidence; retain it across scenarios rather than recomputing from study
bills. An account/year with no verified assignment rejects.

Current 53/5-2, Original, issued/effective March2019, states franchise surcharge
**$0.000620/kWh**, all schedules and special contracts, **bills rendered on/after
January7,2000**. It is added before any surcharge on total bill and in addition
to minimum charges. Industrial 500-million/1,500-million-kWh credits are outside
ordinary R/small-G scope. Do not invent those thresholds in a small account.

There is no separate Pepco-style 2.0408% bill multiplier in the recovered PE
retail schedule. EmPOWER calculation 56/5-5 explicitly includes GRT and PSC
assessment adjustments in its published rate; do not add these again. The
Cogeneration PURPA termination surcharge filing likewise includes GRT/assessment
in its revenue requirement (owned by delivery research). Source bill definitions
list franchise as the tax based on electricity delivered. Use the company's
filed component amounts; no wholesale price, LMP, PLC/NSPL or inferred extra
GRT/PSC line is justified by the tax's existence alone.

Current 55/5-4, **Sixth Revision**, issued June30,2026, explicitly sets MES at
**$0.000149/kWh**, maximum $1,000/month, effective **bills rendered on/after
July1,2026**. It is not $0.000150 by neighboring-utility analogy. The tariff
expressly excludes MES from Maryland sales tax, Montgomery County local tax,
late-payment charges and utility revenue. Preserve bill-rendered/service-rendered
clocks as independent facts. Matching complete calendar cycles with bill date in
the same designated month can use this factor from July2026; shifted cycles
require independent allocation evidence.

The older SOS-directory book 55/5-4, Fourth Revision, sets $0.000150 effective
bills rendered July1,2022. An indexed web copy of Fifth Revision describes a
July1,2025 value **$0.000159**, but the original fifth-revision PDF was **not**
recovered: the static current URL now yields Sixth Revision. This web-only
historical observation is not a pinned executable 2025–June2026 rate and must
not fill a gap. Statewide statutory maximum alone cannot establish an actual
provider's billed rate for a missing date.

[Public Utilities §7-203](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gpu&section=7-203)
caps the assessment at lesser of $0.00015/kWh or $1,000/month/account. The verified
PE $0.000149 factor is below that per-kWh maximum. [COMAR20.50.01.04](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/20.50.01.04)
requires **cent rounding with half-cent upward** for MES. Example: 1,000 kWh ×
$0.000149 = $0.149 raw, **$0.15 billed**. A raw linear objective is not an exact
rounded-invoice objective; disclose/bound the difference rather than claiming
its bill optimum automatically proves a rounded invoice optimum. Small study
limits such as R<=7,500/G<=6,000 kWh keep the $1,000 monthly cap inactive; larger
account cap treatment remains separate.

## State sales tax, RRC and legislative refunds

[COMAR03.06.01.10](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/03.06.01.10)
D1 exempts electricity on filed residential/domestic schedules, applicable to
PE R. Ordinary commercial G electricity and delivery remain taxable absent a
verified exemption. C1–C3 exclude late penalties, county tax and MES from taxable
price; PE's own MES rider explicitly confirms its exemption. [COMAR03.06.01.08](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/03.06.01.08)
and Tax-General11-101 govern included vendor taxes/discounts. [Tax-General11-104(a)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=11-104)
requires approximately-6% brackets, not ordinary rounding of 6% (taxable$100.01
=> tax$6.01). No independently reconciled ordinary PE G tax-base/bracket invoice
was recovered. A first G subtotal should explicitly exclude **unmodeled state
sales tax**, not represent zero tax or a business exemption.

The official [2026 adjustment sheet](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/content/dam/customer/Customer%20Choice/Files/maryland/tariffs/PotomacEdisonaceca.pdf),
physical1, explicitly fills RRC cells Jan–Dec2026 with accounting **$ -**,
$ per residential Schedule-R customer. Normalize these literal currency dashes
as **$0**, retaining the original notation/provenance; they are not blank cells.
The sheet explicitly makes RRC effective on the first of each month on a
**service-rendered** basis. No unrecovered 2025 credit or future sheet may be
silently inferred from this 2026 table. G is not eligible for the residential RRC.

[PE's Aug28,2025 announcement](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/newsroom/news_articles/pe-resi-customers-in-md-to-receieve-relief-refund-bill-credits.html)
confirms first legislative credit **September2025**; first-round eligibility is
active residential account June1,2025, recorded usage Apr1,2024–Mar31,2025, active
account when credited. Exact first-round award amounts were not recovered.
[PE's current relief FAQ](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/help/maryland-energy-relief-credit.html)
confirms second posting **February2026**, based on historic average usage from
Sep2024–Aug2025. Its table gives below-570-kWh usage tier $45.32; 570–1705-kWh tier
$52.82; >1705-kWh tier $60.32. The tier values are kWh, not dollar thresholds.
Eligibility requires active account Nov1,2025, recorded usage Sep1,2024–Aug31,
2025, and active account in posting month. Do not derive a historical award from
optimized future imports. Use account award/posting evidence if modeled.

**Completeness decision:** ordinary R **September–October2026** can have complete
recurring tariff-component coverage, conditional on all supply/delivery sources
being complete, literal RRC zero, residential sales-tax exemption and independently
confirmed absence of opening credit bank/adjustments or special charges. The
known legislative posting months are outside this window; an unnecessary relief
exclusion should not be carried into it. Exact cent rounding/dispatch-objective
claims remain separately qualified. G remains a clearly disclosed subtotal excluding both unresolved state sales
tax and the unverified transmission minimum/capacity component; only its
independently verified transmission energy blocks are included. Distribution
ordinary-branch arithmetic has now been independently corroborated by CURRENT
columns in the official September4,2026 rate-case filing (below). Earlier unsupported
dates reject rather than use the latest tax/rider factors.

## Cycles, Type I and ordinary PV/storage

Current22/4-13 Rule10(d), effectiveJan1,2026: monthly bills as near30days as
practicable; periods below25 or above35days prorate against30days. Complete
28/29/30/31-day calendar cycles therefore use one monthly fixed charge. Rules
10(b–c) permit utility estimated/bimonthly billing in nondemand cases; first scope
must independently confirm actual regular matching monthly cycles. A generic
proration clause does not prove allocation across every dated rate change.

Current12/4-3, effectiveApr1,2025: G TypeI requires prior-year history without
metered **30-minute** billing demand >=25kW, consumption >6,000kWh in any two
consecutive nonsummer months, or >7,500kWh in one summer month. **SummerJune–
September; nonsummerOctober–May.** G71/7-2 says utility categorizes in June and
customers do not switch between June–May. Existing type is independently assigned,
not inferred from a representative current load. Current/prior-year calendars,
capacity requirements and wholesale tags are distinct facts.

G71/7-2 without demand meter defines CapacityRequired as
`max(current_month_kWh, 0.5*max(previous_12_month_kWh))/250`. Retain actual history
and reject missing months. It also contains nearest-half-kW language in the
metered-capacity section; no invented tie rule or automatic application to an
unmetered formula is authorized. G72/7-3 short-term MonthlyService adders decline
10/8/6/4/2% to zero from month12. First ordinary scope needs independent no-adder
confirmation or at least12months continuous service, not an implicit brand-new
account assumption. Delivery research owns exact minimum/floor reconciliation.

G69/7 excludes Standby/Maintenance service for alternative generation facilities;
Rule23 50/4-41 directs backup/maintenance to AGS. AGS147/20 is a separate schedule,
not a borrowed small-generation exemption. The NEM rider current164/26 and older
159/26 Original(2019) give identical ordinary energy, capacity, reactive, rate,
monthly and minimum charges, and explicitly give **no adjustment to demand
billing determinant/capacity charge**. Current165/26-1 defines net energy as
company supply minus exports and bills positive net energy under ordinary terms.
Thus zero exports imply net energy equals imports, but NEM does not reset native
capacity or prior history or grant a new capacity discount.

For G DER require express utility approval retaining the same unmetered billing
method across scenarios, with unchanged assigned minimum and valid prior12month
history. Do not reset history to a newly generated PV load. For R/G require
approved ordinary NEM/parallel PV-plus-storage service, no AGS/special charges,
zero exports and zero opening bank. Storage must be approved as part of that
specific PV agreement; standalone-storage billing is unverified. No NEM export,
credit-bank, net-excess or cash-settlement implementation is authorized here.

**Fixture-ready tax-only arithmetic**, E=1,000kWh and matching Sep2026 bill:
franchise$0.62, MESraw$0.149/billed$0.15, R USP$0.32/RRC$0, selected G USP$1.84.
R these components total **$1.09 billed**; G **$2.61 before excluded sales tax**.
These are independent component fixtures, not a whole customer-bill benchmark.


## Final conditional G domain and independent distribution fixtures

Delivery research preserved the [September4,2026 official rate-case filing](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/content/dam/customer/Customer%20Choice/Files/maryland/tariffs/PE-MD%202026-09-04%20Base%20Rate%20Filing.pdf)
(`delivery/base_case_2026_filing.pdf`). Use only ExhibitRV-13 **CURRENT** columns,
not proposed rates. Physical172 R E=1,000 gives distribution$28.87, matching
$6+.02287×1,000. Physical173 G5kW/E730 gives$21.22, matching$4.85+.02243×730;
an additional$1.70×5 would contradict that primary benchmark. G10kW/E1,460
CURRENT$42.90 matches$4.85+.02243×1,460+2.12×(10−7.5). This establishes ordinary
current distribution arithmetic; it is not an active-floor or transmission
minimum benchmark and does not establish a complete G invoice.

The final narrow G proposal retains utility-confirmed absence of a demand meter,
actual 30-minute import peak below7.5kW, unchanged assigned/native minimum and
complete prior12month energy history. Let C=max(E,0.5×priorMaxE)/250. Numerically
accept only when the verified regular-variable charge .02243×E is at least
1.70×(C+.25); the quarter-kW margin conservatively bounds nearest-half-kW
rounding without selecting an unstated tie rule. Also reject any unsupported
additional minimum/contract condition. This guard is an **accept/reject scope
check after solving**, not a minimum-import optimizer constraint: it must not
force extra imports to obtain a supported bill. If the relaxation produces an
active/uncertain-floor case, report it unsupported rather than claiming verified
savings. Preserve history across grid/PV/battery and require utility approval
retaining the unmetered method. Transmission capacity/minimum and state sales
tax remain visibly excluded; no complete-bill cost optimum is claimed.
