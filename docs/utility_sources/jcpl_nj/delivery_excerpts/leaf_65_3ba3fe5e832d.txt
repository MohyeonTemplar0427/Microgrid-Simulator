BPU No. 14 ELECTRIC - PART III      Original Sheet No. 65 
 
APPLICABILITY:  The Electric Generation/Capacity Cost Rider (“Rider EGCC”) provides for a  bill 
credit and charge to mitigate the increase in generation supply cost during July and August 2025 to 
all Full Service and Delivery Service residential customers under Service Classification RS, RT and 
RGT. 
 
Bill Credit/Charge Per Billing Month (includes Sales and Use Tax as provided in Rider SUT) 
 
      Electric Generation/Capacity Cost Deferral 
July and August of 2025 - Credit     $30 
 
      Electric Generation/Capacity Cost Deferral Recovery 
September 2025 to February 2026 - Charge    $10 
 
 
The Rider EGCC  deferred balance shall be tracked every month without carry ing charges. The 
balance of the EGCC regulatory asset as of March 1, 2026 will be rolled into the Non-Utility Generation 
Charge (NGC) Rider clause balance for recovery.
