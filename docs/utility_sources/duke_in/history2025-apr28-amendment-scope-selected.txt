RESPONSE TO DUKE INDUSTRIAL GROUP AND OUCC APRIL 22, 2025  
REQUEST FOR RULING 
 
Duke Energy Indiana, LLC (“Duke Energy Indiana”), by counsel, hereby responds to the 
Duke Industrial Group (“Industrial Group”) and the Indiana Office of Utility Consumer 
Counselor (“OUCC”) Request for Ruling filed April 22, 2025 (“Request”) as follows: 

PDF PAGE 2
Contrary to the arguments of the OUCC and Industrial Group, the Commission need not 
do anything in response to the Request because the Commission has already ruled on the matters 
they raise. As the Commission noted in its January 29, 2025 Order, “a 30-day review period for 
Petitioner’s Step 2 compliance filing is appropriate, and that review period is not necessary for 
the Step 1 compliance filing.” Cause No. 46038 Order at 85. The Commission then declined to 
reconsider its decision regarding a review period after the Step 1 Compliance Filing, stating that 
“While the OUCC recommended the Commission provide the parties with at least 60 days to 
review Duke’s compliance filing with updated rate base and capital structure, Duke did not 
propose to update its rate base or capital structure in its Step 1 compliance filing. OUCC Exhibit 
1 at 20; 46038 Order at 85. No other party requested or recommended a review period.” Cause 
No. 46038 Order on Reconsideration at 2. Furthermore, Duke Energy Indiana’s Step 1 
Compliance Filing was approved on February 25, 2025 subject to the outcome of the then-
pending Petitions for Rehearing. The Commission denied both Petitions for Rehearing on 
April 16, 2025, thus ruling on the pending matters before it. 
In accordance with the Commission’s April 9, 2025 Nunc Pro Tunc Order, Duke Energy 
Indiana intends to file updated LLF and HLF Tariffs with the Commission on or before May 2, 
2025. 
 In conclusion, Duke Energy Indiana suggests that the Commission ignore the OUCC and 
Industrial Group’s request to rule again on their issues that have already been ruled upon.
