On April 30, 2025, Duke Energy Indiana, LLC (“Applicant”) filed its Verified Application 
for approval by the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission (“Commission”) of a change in its fuel 
adjustment charge (“FAC”) , to be applicable during the billing cycles of July, August, and 
September 2025 for electric and steam service. Concurrently, on April 30, 2025, Applicant 
submitted its prefiled case-in-chief testimony and attachments under this Cause. 
 
On May 16, 2025, the Duke Energy Indiana Industrial Group (“Industrial Group”) filed its 
Petition to Intervene in this proceeding, which was subsequently granted on May 23, 2025. 
 
On June 4, 2025, t he Indiana Office of Utility Consumer Counselor (“OUCC”) filed its 
testimony and attachments.  
 
On June 4, 2025, Industrial Group filed its testimony and attachments. 
 
Applicant filed its rebuttal testimony on June 9, 2025.   
 
 The Commission held an evidentiary hearing on June 12, 2025, at 1:00 p.m. in Room 224 
of the PNC Center, 101 West Washington Street, Indianapolis, Indiana . Applicant, OUCC, and 
Industrial Group participated in  the hearing by counsel, during which the ir respective prefiled  
testimony and attachments were admitted into the evidentiary record without objection.

 Ms. Graft testified that , as directed in the Commission’s Order in Cause No. 45508,

 Ms. Graft testified that the Commission authorized Applicant to execute the Speedway 
Solar purchase power agreement (“PPA”) in its Order in Cause No. 45907. The underlying project 
is expected to be operational in mid-summer 2025, at which time Applicant will begin recovering 
the retail portion of the PPA costs through the FAC proceedings, similar to other PPAs previously 
approved by the Commission. She also stated that the Commission authorized Applicant to recover 
its expenses associated with entering into the Speedway Solar PPA of $129,024 over a three-year 
period through the FAC proceedings. She testified that the native load fuel cost includes a monthly 
amortization of $3,584 that began in November 2023 and continues through October 2026.  
  
 Mr. Guerrettaz testified that Applicant’s  fuel cost adjustment for the quarter ended 
February 2025 had been properly applied by Applicant . He further testified that while Applicant 
used forecasted market prices for its natural gas and purchased power as of April 1, 2025 for its 
proposed FAC factor, the market experienced a 10 -15% decrease in future purchased power and

using more current purchased power and natural gas prices . He testified that this high- level 
estimate model rerun produced a reduced factor  of 2.969 mills per KWh as compared to 
Applicant’s 4.042 mills per KWh.
