 On October 31, 2024, Duke Energy Indiana, LLC (“Duke Energy Indiana” or “Applicant”) 
filed its Verified Application and direct testimony and exhibits for approval by the Indiana Utility 
Regulatory Commission (“Commission”) of a change in its fuel adjustment charge (“FAC”) to be 
applicable during the billing cycles of January, February, and March 2025 for electric and steam 
service. On November 19, 2024, Duke Energy Indiana filed the revised direct testimony of James 
J. McClay, III, Managing Director of Natural Gas Trading for Duke Energy Corporation.   
 
On December 5, 2024, the Indiana Office of Utility Consumer Counselor (“OUCC”) filed 
its audit report and testimony, and, on December 12, 2024, Applicant filed rebuttal testimony.  
 
 An evidentiary hearing was held in this Cause on December  16, 2024 at 9 a.m. in Room 
222 of the PNC Center, 101 West Washington Street, Indianapolis, Indiana . Applicant and the 
OUCC appeared at the hearing by counsel and offered their respective prefiled testimony into the 
evidentiary record without objection. 
  
 Based upon the applicable law and the evidence of record , the Commission now finds:  
 
1. Notice a nd Commission Jurisdiction . Notice of the hearing in this Cause was 
given as required by law. Applicant is a public utility within the meaning of Ind . Code § 8-1-2-
1(a). Under Ind. Code § 8-1-2-42, the Commission has jurisdiction over changes to Applicant ’s 
rates and charges related to adjustments in fuel costs. Therefore, the Commission has jurisdiction 
over the parties and the subject matter of this Cause.
