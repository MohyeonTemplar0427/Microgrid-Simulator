Rider 8 printed 98 / web P106 (zero-based), supplement 92, effective 2025-06-01:
Company evaluates the customer's 12 most recent monthly billing demands available in October of the preceding year. An average below 25 kW is assigned small C&I supply effective with January billing. Classification is not inferred from current PV-reduced imports.
Printed 102 / web P113: DSS procurement includes energy, capacity, transmission/distribution line losses, congestion, AECs, ancillary services, PJM management charges and ARRs/FTRs. DSS excludes transmission service within the Duquesne zone; Appendix A supplies that component separately.
Printed 103 / web P114: DSS is filed at least 60 days before the next application period. Rates reconcile every six months. The filed DSS rate is rounded to the nearest 0.001 mill/kWh and applied to all kWh billed for default service during the billing month. Formula: [(CA + (SLR + DSSa + E)/Sa) * F + DSSb/Sb] * [1/(1-T)]. DSSa already includes procurement, administration, plan filing and working-capital expenses.
Printed 104 / web P115: Residential rate factor 1.0066 for June 2025-May 2026 and June 2026-May 2027; all other customer groups use 1.0. This factor is already incorporated in the filed DSS rates and is not an additional bill multiplier.
