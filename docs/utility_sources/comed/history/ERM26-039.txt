Informational Sheet No. 36.1 is being filed in accordance with Rider CFRA – Carbon-Free Resource 
Adjustments (Rider CFRA) to update the non-summer Carbon-Free Resource (CFR) Adjustment 
effective beginning with the April 2026 monthly billing period to include the Carbon-Free Resource 
Monthly Reconciliation (CFRMR) Factor effective in April 2026 based on December 2025 actuals.  
Included in the CFRMR for April 2026 are the Production Tax Credits (PTCs) totaling ($115 million) that 
reflect the amount to be refunded to customers from the Carbon-Free Energy Resources’ tax returns 
per the Illinois Power Agency (IPA) for the determination period of December 2025.  The total 
calculated value of the CFR Adjustment and CFRMR is a credit of (3.186) cents per kilowatt-hour for 
April 2026.
