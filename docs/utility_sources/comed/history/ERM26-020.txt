Informational Sheet No. 36.1 is being filed in accordance with Rider CFRA – Carbon-Free Resource 
Adjustments (Rider CFRA) to update the non-summer Carbon-Free Resource (CFR) Adjustment 
effective beginning with the March 2026 monthly billing period to include the Carbon-Free Resource 
Monthly Reconciliation (CFRMR) Factor effective in March 2026 based on November 2025 actuals.  
Included in the CFRMR for March 2026 are the Production Tax Credits (PTCs) totaling ($115 million) 
that reflect the amount to be refunded to customers from the Carbon-Free Energy Resources’ tax 
returns per the Illinois Power Agency (IPA) for the determination period of November 2025.  
Furthermore, the CFRMR for March 2026 also includes a one-time credit to change from 0 to 63.32 
revenue lag days in the Cash Working Capital calculation for the determination periods of June 2025 
through October 2025 plus applicable interest of ($5 million) .  The total calculated value of the CFR 
Adjustment and CFRMR is a credit of (4.110) cents per kilowatt-hour for March 2026.
