9th Revised Informational Sheet No. 63 canceling 8th Revised Informational Sheet No. 63 
 
The revised informational sheet is to become effective on April 18, 2026. The calculated value 
of the Coal to Solar Adjustment (CTSA), beginning with the May 2026 monthly billing period, will 
increase to 0.009 cents per kWh (¢/kWh) to provide for a better match between the expected 
costs of purchasing Coal to Solar Renewable Energy Credits, resulting from an additional 
project activated in March 2026, and the recovery of those costs. This value includes the zero-
balancing adjustment in accordance with the provisions of Rider CTS.
