ComEd is submitting this informational filing for Rider LID – Low-Income Discount (Rider LID), 
to update the Low-Income Discount (LID) percentages and Low-Income Discount Recovery 
(LIDR) charges based on the 2026 Federal Poverty Guidelines and the updated number of low-
income customers receiving discounts. This filing updates the applicable rates in accordance 
with the provisions of Rider LID.  
 
The informational sheets will become effective January 21, 2026, and the LIDs and LIDRs will 
become effective beginning with the February 2026 monthly billing period.
