The first update relates to revisions to the Low-Income Discount Recovery (LIDR) charges to 
align projected recovery with expected program discounts. These revisions reflect increased 
enrollment of eligible low-income customers, including continued enrollment growth associated 
with recent marketing efforts and press coverage of the LID program. 
 
The second update is housekeeping in nature. Following discussions with ICC Staff, ComEd 
identified that the effective dates for the LID percentages should be stated as effective through 
March 2027 rather than December 2026. The next update to the LID percentages is expected to 
be filed in January 2027 and effective on February 2027 bills following the annual update to the 
Federal Poverty Level (FPL). Extending the expiration date through March 2027 provides 
additional flexibility in the event of a delay in publication of the updated FPL. This housekeeping 
change is consistent with the provisions of Rider LID and the Commission’s Final Order and 
Amendatory Order in Docket No. 24-0163.
