Pursuant to the provisions of Rider DSPR – Delivery Service Pricing Reconciliation (Rider 
DSPR) that allow ComEd to revise Delivery Reconciliation Adjustment Factor (DRAF) listed in 
Informational Sheet No. 9 to better match the Delivery Reconciliation Amount (DRA) of

17.0696% to 10.8072%.

effective beginning with the November 2025 monthly billing period.
