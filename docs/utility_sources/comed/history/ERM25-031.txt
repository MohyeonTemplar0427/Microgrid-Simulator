Coal to Solar Adjustment 
4th Revised Informational Sheet No. 63 canceling 3rd Revised Informational Sheet No. 63 
 
The revised informational sheet is to become effective on March 18, 2025. The calculated value 
of the Coal to Solar Adjustment (CTSA), beginning with the April 2025 monthly billing period, is 
(0.0009) cents per kWh (¢/kWh). However, upon review of ComEd’s applicable costs, recovery 
of those costs, and the uncertainty related to future additional Renewable Energy Credit (REC) 
costs, ComEd will keep the value of the CTSA at 0.0000 ¢/kWh.  
 
At this point in time, the amount that ComEd is required to collect for the procurement of RECs 
does not exceed the $868,250 credit balance on the rider due to forfeited collateral from 
counterparties that canceled REC contracts in 2023.  Also, ComEd is required to collect $0 to 
fund the Coal to Solar Energy Storage Grant Initiative Fund in 2025. Furthermore, the balancing 
amounts applicable to the CTSA remain at 0.0000 ¢/kWh and any credits associated with the 
forfeited collateral recorded in 2024 will be used to offset future costs to ComEd retail 
customers.
