## Billing cycles, effective clocks and rounding

GeneralInformation7 leaf10A physical13 defines an ordinary monthly billing period as **26–34days**. Periods outside that range prorate service-classification rates by thirtieths of a monthly period. Thus regular complete28/29/30/31-local-day calendarcycles can retain full monthly charges/blocks, subject to verified account billing-month assignment and component clocks. Do not borrow ACE's exact30-day rule or JCP&L's35-day limit. Initial scope excludes partial, shifted and short-term periods requiring unverified detailed proration.

No Rockland equivalent of JCP&L PartII3.07 was established in GeneralInformation12: it merely says serviceclassifications are subject to lawful changes. The adopted2021 order expressly says service rendered from January1,2022; the2026 order's scannedphysical10 expressly says service rendered on/after July1,2026. Use each component's filed clock. BGS reconciliation separately applies to usage **billed in the specifiedmonth**; it must not be split by service intervals merely because a quarter starts during a meter cycle. Billing month is an explicit account fact. Seasonal non-TOD labels say summer billingmonths June–September. A complete-calendar study with matching final-day bill/month avoids importing an actual customer's different reading schedule.

No ordinary SC1/SC2 kWh rounding quantum, cash-cent convention or exact demand half-tie rule was established.
