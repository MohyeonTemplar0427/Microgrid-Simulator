GENERAL INFORMATION 
 
      
No. 35  ELECTRIC GENERATION/CAPACITY COST DEFERRAL AND RECOVERY 
  
 
For the two (2)-month period beginning July through August 2025, the Company will apply an Electric 
Generation/Capacity Cost Deferral monthly bill credit of $30.00, including Sales and Use Tax (“SUT”) 
to each full-service and retail access residential customer served under this schedule.  For the six (6)-
month period beginning September 2025 through February 2026, the Company will apply an Electric 
Generation/Capacity Cost Deferral Recovery monthly bill charge of $10.00, including SUT to each 
residential full-service and retail access customer served under this schedule.  Any under- or over-
recovery balances will be collected from, or returned to, customers via the Company’s February 1, 
2026, Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (“RGGI”) Surcharge filing, without interest.
