Sheets 22 (substitute fourteenth revised) and 23 (substitute twelfth revised); issued September 20, 2024; effective October 1, 2024.
One nonresidential establishment adjacent to a distribution circuit of adequate capacity. The tariff uses a less-than-20,000 kWh criterion for 12 consecutive billing months; section 2.03 generally relates classification to monthly energy. Preserve utility-confirmed classification/history, rather than treating this as an annual 20,000 kWh limit. Existing enrolled customers may continue despite exceeding the criterion. No resale.
Service: 60 Hz, 120/240 V single-phase, one meter, one point of delivery. Alternative service and three-phase require separate facts.
Service charge $0.7562/day/meter. The $23 monthly figure is comparison only.
600 energy: winter $0.12041/kWh; summer $0.16513/kWh.
607 energy: summer on-peak $0.28232/kWh; winter off-peak $0.12041; summer off-peak $0.14116.
Summer is June 1–August 31. On-peak is 2–7 p.m. CST Monday–Friday, excluding Independence Day; other hours off-peak. Minimum TOD term one year.
Three-phase incremental charge $45/month or $1.4795/day/meter. No demand charge for 600/607; 707 and larger schedules are distinct.
Riders: EAC, TAX, EEBC, EECR, DRCR, RTS, RCE, TBR. Optional programs, excess facilities and late-payment fees require separate account facts.
