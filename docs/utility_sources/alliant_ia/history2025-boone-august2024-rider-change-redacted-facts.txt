EECR old: 1620 x $0.0018 x 12/33 = $1.06.
EECR new: 1620 x $0.0014 x 21/33 = $1.44.
DRCR old: 1620 x $0.0014 x 12/33 = $0.82.
DRCR new: 1620 x $0.0023 x 21/33 = $2.37.
This verifies a general-service rider transition at August 1, 2024 (21 new-factor days through August 21), corresponding to the known preceding EECR sheet's August 1 body date and the old DRCR factor used in early 2025. It provides primary actual-bill evidence despite the missing original substitute-sixth DRCR tariff sheet. It does not establish the residential class's factor or applicability.
