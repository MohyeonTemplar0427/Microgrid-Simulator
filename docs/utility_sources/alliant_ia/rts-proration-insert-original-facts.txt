Official utility RTS bill insert, document dated2017, explains that a cycle crossing an effective date may show prorated old and new RTS charges based on billing date and rate effective date. It is application-rule evidence, not independent2025 price evidence. The2025 actual government-published crossing-cycle bill corroborates total-cycle kWh multiplied by old/new day fractions. Existing complete-calendar2025 studies need no within-month RTS split because the rate changes September1. Arbitrary meter-cycle endpoint and rounding rules require separate validation.
