Factual extraction / interpretation notes (not a complete tariff transcription):
Iowa general sales tax is 6%. Electricity is sourced to the billed subscriber address. Residential energy is state-tax exempt but subject to 1% local energy excise in LOST jurisdictions. Electricity subject to a city/county franchise or user fee is exempt from LOST.
