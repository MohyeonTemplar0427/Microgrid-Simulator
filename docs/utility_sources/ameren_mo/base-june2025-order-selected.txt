ORDER APPROVING COMPLIANCE TARIFF SHEETS
Issue Date: May 13, 2025 Effective Date: June 1, 2025
On April 23, 2025, the Commission issued its Order Approving Stipulations and 
Agreements, which approved two stipulation and agreements resolving this general rate 
case. That order rejected the tariff sheets Union Electric Company d/b/a Ameren Missouri
filed to initiate this general rate case and directed Ameren Missouri to file tariff sheets in 
compliance with the two approved stipulation and agreements. On May 2, 2025, Ameren 
Missouri filed compliance tariff sheets, Tracking No. JE -2025-0150.
The Commission directed its staff to file a recommendation addressing Ameren 
Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets by May 12, 2025. Other parties were also given until 
May 12, 2025, to provide comments on Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets. Staff 
filed a recommendation on May 12, 2025, that the Commission approve Ameren 
Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets. The Commission received no other comments about 
Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets.
The Commission’s Order Approving Stipulations and Agreements delegated 
authority to the Regulatory Law Judge to approve Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff 
sheets and decide if good cause exists for the tariff to take effect in less than thirty days.
The Stipulation and Agreement filed on March 14, 2025, and approved by the 
Commission, provided that compliance tariff sheets are to become effective on

June 1, 2025. Therefore, good cause exists for Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff 
sheets to take effect in less than thirty days. 
Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets submitted under Tracking No. 
JE-2025-0150 on April 23, 2025, and tariff sheet 92.3, as substituted on May 12, 2025, 
will be approved to become effective with less than thirty days’ notice. So that this order 
and Ameren Missouri’s compliance tariff sheets are concurrently effective, the Regulatory 
Law Judge finds it reasonable to make this order effective in less than thirty days.
THE COMMISSION ORDERS THAT:
1. The compliance tariff sheets submitted under Tracking No. JE-2025-0150 
on May 2, 2025, and tariff sheet 92.3 as substituted on May 12, 2025, are approved to be 
effective on and after June 1, 2025.
2. This order shall become effective on June 1, 2025.
