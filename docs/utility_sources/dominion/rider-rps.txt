Virginia Electric and Power Company



RIDER RPS

I. APPLICABILITY & AVAILABILITY

This rider is applicable to all retail Customers of the Company located in the
Commonwealth as a non- bypassable charge, irrespective of the generation supplier of such
Customer, pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-585.5 F, and notwithstanding any other tariff provision
or contract term applicable to such Customer, except for the following:
A. A Customer meeting the accelerated renewable energy buyer requirements pursuant
to Virginia Code § 56-585.5 G; and
B. A Customer with a peak demand in calendar year 2019 that exceeded 100 MW and
that elected to purchase Electricity Supply Service from a Competitive Service
Provider prior to April 1, 2019 pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-577 A 3.
II. MONTHLY RATE

The charge for service under Virginia Electric and Power Company's Filed Rate Schedules
1, 1G, 1P, 1S, 1T, 1W, DP-R, 1EV, EV, 5, 5C, 5P, 6, GS-1, DP-1, GS-2, DP-2, GS-2T, GS-3,
GS-3 EV, GS -4, GS-5, MBR, SCR, 6TS, 7, 8, 10, 24, 25, 27, 28 and 29, as well as applicable
energy charges specified in any special rates, contracts or incentives approved by the State
Corporation Commission pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-235.2 shall be increased by 0.5520 cents
per kilowatt-hour.
