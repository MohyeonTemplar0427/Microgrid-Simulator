RIDER CCR
CLOSURE OF COAL COMBUSTION RESIDUALS IMPOUNDMENTS

I. APPLICABILITY
 This rider is applicable to all retail  Customers of the Company located in the
Commonwealth as a non- bypassable charge, irrespective of the generation supplier of such
Customer, pursuant to Senate Bill 1355.1. H. and Virginia Code § 56- 585.1.A.5.e., and
notwithstanding any other tariff provision or contract term applicable to such Customer.
II. MONTHLY RATE
 The charge for service under Virginia Electric and Power Company's Filed Rate Schedules
1, 1G, 1P, 1S, 1T, 1W, DP-R, 1EV, EV, 5, 5C, 5P, 6, GS -1, DP-1, GS-2, DP-2, GS-2T, GS-3,
GS- 3 EV, GS- 4, MBR, SCR, 6TS, 7, 8, 10, 24, 25, 27, 28 and 29, as well as applicable energy
charges specified in any special rates, contracts or incentives approved by the State Corporation
Commission pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-235.2 shall be increased by 0.1765 cents per kilowatt-
hour.
