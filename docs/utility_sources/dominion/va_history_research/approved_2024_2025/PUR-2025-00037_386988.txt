On November 25, 2025, the State Corporation Commission of Virginia (“Commission”) issued its 
Final Order approving, among other things, the Rider CERC rate adjustment clause. On 
December 2, 2025, the Commission’s Staff accepted Virginia Electric and Power Company’s 
(“Company”) Rider CERC tariff sheet effective for usage on and after January 1, 2026. On 
December 15, 2025, Appalachian Voices, the National Association for the Advancement of 
Colored People, and Mothers Out Front filed a Petition for Reconsideration (“Petition”). On 
December 16, 2025, the Commission granted the Petition and suspended the Final Order. On 
December 29, 2025, the Commission issued an order directing the Company to file a limited 
response addressing the Petition’s claims. In the Company’s Response to Petition for 
Reconsideration, the Company noted that unless otherwise directed by the Commission, in the 
event the Petition is denied and the Final Order reinstated, the Company intends to submit a revised 
compliance filing to Commission Staff for approval, reflecting updated tariff schedules using the 
approved rate design, to recover the authorized revenue requirement over the remainder of the 
approved rate year. On February 12, 2026, the Commission issued its Order on Reconsideration, 
which denied the Petition and reinstated the Final Order.
