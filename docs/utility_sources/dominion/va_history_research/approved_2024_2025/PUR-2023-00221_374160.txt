RIDER RPS
APPLICABILITY & AVAILABILITYI.
This rider is applicable to all retail Customers of the Company located in the
Commonwealth as a non-bypassable charge, irrespective of the generation supplier of such
Customer, pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-585.5 F, and notwithstanding any other tariff provision 
or contract term applicable to such Customer, except for the following:
A Customer meeting the accelerated renewable energy buyer requirements pursuant A
to Virginia Code § 56-585.5 G; and
A Customer with a peak demand in calendar year 2019 that exceeded 100 MW andB.
that elected to purchase Electricity Supply Service from a Competitive Service
Provider prior to April 1, 2019 pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-577 A 3.
II. MONTHLY RATE
The charge for service under Virginia Electric and Power Company's Filed Rate Schedules 
1, 1G, IP, IS, IT, 1W, DP-R, 1EV, EV, 5, 5C, 5P, 6, GS-1, DP-1, GS-2, DP-2, GS-2T, GS-3,
GS-4, MBR, SCR, 6TS, 7, 8, 10, 24, 25, 27, 28 and 29, as well as applicable energy charges 
specified in any special rates, contracts or incentives approved by the State Corporation
Commission pursuant to Virginia Code § 56-235.2 shall be increased by 0.4689 cents per kilowatt- 
hour.
