Virginia Electric and Power Company
2023 BIENNIAL REVIEW CREDIT
In accordance with the Commission’s Final Order in Case No. PUR-2023-00101, the Company
will provide an aggregate amount of bill credits for the 2023 Biennial Review Credit totaling $15 
million to eligible customers. The 2023 Biennial Review Credit will be used for calculating customer 
credits based on the Customer’s Electricity Supply kilowatt-hours purchased from the Company for 
usage on and after January 1, 2024 through and including June 30, 2024. Once such calculations have 
been completed, the 2023 Biennial Review Credit tariff will no longer be effective and will be 
withdrawn. The billing of customer credits will be applied to customer bills by September 30, 2024.
The charge for service under Virginia Electric and Power Company's Filed Rate Schedules 1, 
1G, IP, IS, IT, 1W, DP-R, 1EV, EV, 5, 5C, 5P, 6, GS-1, DP-1, GS-2, DP-2, GS-2T, GS-3, GS-4, MBR,
SCR, 6TS, 7, 8, 10, 24, 25, 27, 28 and 29, as well as applicable energy charges specified in any special 
rates, contracts or incentives approved by the State Corporation Commission pursuant to Virginia Code 
§ 56-235.2 shall be increased by -0.0401 cents per Electricity Supply kilowatt-hour.
