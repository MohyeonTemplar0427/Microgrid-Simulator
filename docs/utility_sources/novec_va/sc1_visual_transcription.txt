NOVEC Small Commercial Service, Schedule SC-1.
Visual transcription from both pages of the official scanned PDF:
https://www.novec.com/About_NOVEC/upload/Small-Commercial-Service-SC-1-3-22-23.pdf
Filed for informational purposes March 22, 2023; effective April 1, 2023.

Page 1:
Single-phase monthly distribution service charge: 26.78 dollars.
Three-phase monthly distribution service charge: 43.58 dollars (outside initial scope).
All delivered energy distribution: 0.02064 dollars/kWh.
All supply energy: 0.09242 dollars/kWh.
Monthly minimum: 26.78 dollars single-phase; 43.58 dollars three-phase.
Two preceding-twelve-month billing demands exceeding 30 kW trigger LP-1
transfer for succeeding twelve months. Rate-change notice is 90 days.

Page 2:
Standard secondary voltage, 60Hz; single- or three-phase service in full tariff.
Single-phase individual motors limited to 10 horsepower.
Company may install demand/energy monitoring when a month's usage exceeds 6000 kWh.
Open-order contract unless a written contract is requested; then at least one year.
PCA-1 adjusts the calculated charges.

Initial implementation selects single phase, independently retained SC-1,
twelve prior actual demands each below 30 kW, native/import power <=29.9 kW,
and native/import monthly energy <=5999 kWh. These are explicit narrower
study limits, not a replacement statement of the full tariff's eligibility.
