1. Projected Over Recovered Tracker Balance 
 
We project a CIP Tracker balance on September 30, 2025 of negative $18.5 million. This balance 
represents an over recovery through the Conservation Cost Recovery Charge (CCRC) and the 
existing electric CIP Adjustment Factor.3   
 
2. Proposed CIP Adjustment Factor 
 
With this filing, we propose to increase the CIP Adjustment Factor from ($0.000389) per kWh to 
$0.001396 per kWh over the October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026 time period. If approved as 
proposed and implemented on October 1, 2025, the average residential electric customer using 616 
kWh per month would pay approximately $0.86 per month.

if the timing of the approval process suggests the implementation of the 2025-2026 CIP Adjustment 
Factor will occur after October 1, 2025. We will update the implementation date and adjust the 
proposed factor to recover the approved revenue requirements over the remaining months of the 
period, through September 2026. 
 
3. Proposed Customer Notice 
 
We propose to implement the below bill message, effective the first month the 2025/2026 CIP 
Adjustment Factor takes effect, notifying customers of the change in their monthly bills, as follows: 
 
Effective Oct. 1, 2025, the Resource Adjustment line item on your bill has increased due to a 
change in the Conservation Improvement Program (CIP) factor. The electric CIP portion of 
the Resource Adjustment is $0.001396 per kilowatt-hour (kWh).
