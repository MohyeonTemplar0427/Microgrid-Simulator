Before the Minnesota Public Utilities Commission 
   
Comments of the Minnesota Department of Commerce 
 
Docket No. E002/M-24-371 
 
 
On November 1, 2024, Northern States Power Company, doing business as Xcel Energy (Xcel or the 
Company) filed a petition in Docket No. E002/M-24-371, requesting the Minnesota Public Utilities 
Commission (Commission) approve Xcel’s 2025 Transmission Cost Recovery (TCR) Rider revenue 
requirements and the resulting adjustment factors by customer class (Petition).  Xcel’s proposal would 
reduce the total revenue requirements from $62.7 million for 2024 to $57.5 million for 2025, and 
change the TCR Rider factors by category, as shown in Table 1 below, effective January 1, 2025.

Xcel proposed an implementation date of January 1, 2025, stating, using the beginning of the calendar 
year will simplify the rate change process, make the changes clearer to customers, and keep the TCR 
tracker balance more in-line with actual costs.2  Xcel proposes to provisionally implement this TCR 
adjustment factor reduction if it is not possible for the full TCR rider petition to be approved by that 
time.  The January 1, 2025 implementation date for the TCR Rider factors would coincide with the 
implementation of interim rates in Xcel’s multi-year rate case in Docket No. E002/GR-24-320.    
 
Having completed an initial review of Xcel’s Petition, the Department believes full Department review 
and Commission approval is unlikely by January 1, 2025.  Of note, this year’s petition introduces new 
projects.3 Further, the Department does not want to delay implementation of the rate change given it 
is an overall reduction.   
 
The Department therefore recommends the Commission approve Xcel’s requested TCR Rider factors 
provisional rate reduction to be implemented on January 1, 2025, subject to future update.
