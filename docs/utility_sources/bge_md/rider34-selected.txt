Electric – Retail – Baltimore Gas and Electric Company 119
34. Base Distribution Revenue Offset Rider
The Base Distribution Revenue Offset Rider serves in a Multi-Year Plan to provide distribution
customers with accelerated tax benefits, when available and as approved by the Commission, as a
reduction to distribution base rates.
The Base Distribution Revenue Offset Rider revenues and rates shall be included in base
distribution revenues and base distribution rates for the purposes of calculating the test year base
revenues, the change in revenues, and actual revenues in the electric Rider 25 – Monthly Rate Adjustment
for applicable Rate Schedules.
The following Base Distribution Offset Rider rates will be effective for usage January 1, 2024 –
December 31, 2024.
