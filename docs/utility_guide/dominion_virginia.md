## Introduction and service area

Dominion Energy Virginia combines electricity generation, transmission, and distribution. Its retail territory and PJM’s Dominion transmission zone are different concepts: a zone can include other utilities’ customers.

## What makes this service distinctive

Virginia’s account rules must be kept separate from Dominion’s North Carolina rules. For a commercial battery study, measured demand, on-peak demand, and contracted distribution demand can produce different savings. Do not assume reducing one peak reduces every demand-charge component.

Official background: [Dominion operating segments](https://www.dominionenergy.com/about/our-company/operating-segments). [Official retail service locator](https://www.dominionenergy.com/economic-development/virginia/service-area-locator).

## Using this provider in the simulator

An existing local candidate covers a bounded Henrico County commercial GS-2T, approved non-export parallel-service case with billing, dispatch, and representative replay. The broader residential/commercial expansion is in progress and is not counted as completed coverage in this guide.

The existing candidate does not establish a complete historical year, residential service, general Virginia locality coverage, or Dominion North Carolina coverage. Use its documented dates and account contract only.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
