## Introduction and service area

Silicon Valley Power (SVP) is the municipal electric utility for the City of Santa Clara. It is different from Silicon Valley Clean Energy, the community-choice supplier serving other nearby communities.

## What makes this service distinctive

Santa Clara has a substantial data-center presence, which makes continuous commercial demand an important regional study case. A household and a large computing facility should not share a generic daily load curve. City boundaries also matter: being in Santa Clara County does not establish SVP service.

Official background: [About SVP](https://www.siliconvalleypower.com/svp-and-community/about-svp?navid=1). [Data centers in Santa Clara](https://www.siliconvalleypower.com/businesses/data-centers-in-santa-clara).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected D-1, C-1, CB-1, and CB-3 import arrangements, including supported TOU options, feed billing and battery optimization in the main browser. Eligible-window demand and historical demand rules are retained.

Large special-contract schedules, onsite-generation/standby arrangements, and solar export are not covered by this path. Certain power-factor threshold cases can be billed but are unavailable for optimization.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
