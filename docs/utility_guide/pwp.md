## Introduction and service area

Pasadena Water and Power (PWP) is a community-owned utility whose electric service is within Pasadena. Its water service reaches some adjacent communities; the water and electric boundaries should not be treated as identical.

## What makes this service distinctive

Local governance and local electric schedules distinguish PWP from surrounding SCE service. Meter and enrollment status matter: a legacy flat-rate account and a newer interval-meter account need not qualify for the same plan.

Official background: [Who PWP serves](https://pwp.cityofpasadena.net/whoweare/). [Current rate information](https://pwp.cityofpasadena.net/water-and-electric-rates/).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Legacy flat R-1, R-2, and small-commercial S-1 imports are implemented for a narrow June–July 2026 window, through the shared bill, dispatch, and browser path.

TOU/critical-peak, demand-metered commercial families, solar settlement, and special proration are not covered. Support for one legacy plan is not evidence that the current plan offered to a new customer is available.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
