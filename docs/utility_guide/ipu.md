## Introduction and service area

Industry Public Utilities (IPU) operates an electric system serving portions of the business and residential properties in City of Industry. It does not automatically serve every address in the city.

## What makes this service distinctive

This is an important example of a partial-city service area. Confirm the property’s actual utility and contract instead of assigning service from a city name or assuming SCE wherever a map is unclear.

Official background: [Official IPU introduction](https://www.cityofindustry.org/190/Industry-Public-Utilities).

## Using this provider in the simulator

The individual domestic D arrangement is implemented for confirmed ordinary accounts in the shared Southern California billing, dispatch, and browser workflow.

The utility serves businesses in reality, but commercial contract and seasonal rules are not implemented in this adapter. Domestic support must not be presented as commercial support. Solar and special contracts remain excluded.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
