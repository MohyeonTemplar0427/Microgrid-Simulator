## Introduction and service area

CleanPowerSF is SFPUC’s public community-choice electricity program. It purchases electricity for participating San Francisco customers; PG&E continues to deliver it over the local wires. It is not the same account arrangement as Hetch Hetchy Power.

## What makes this service distinctive

A participating customer’s bill combines the provider’s generation charges with applicable PG&E delivery and other charges. Compare that combined bill with bundled service, rather than comparing generation prices alone. Confirm actual enrollment and product from the account.

Official background: [How CleanPowerSF and PG&E work together](https://cleanpowersf.org/faq).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Standard secondary-voltage commercial B-1 combinations are implemented for Green and SuperGreen, with phase and explicit PCIA vintage selections. The shared tariff catalog feeds the browser and location-based dispatch.

This provider’s implemented catalog is commercial B-1, not a claim of residential coverage. Taxes, special exemptions, standby, and solar export settlement are excluded from this adapter.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
