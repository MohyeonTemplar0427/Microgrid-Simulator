## Introduction and service area

Azusa Light and Water (ALW) operates the City of Azusa’s electric utility. The city publishes separate water and electric service maps; water service in neighboring communities does not prove ALW electric service.

## What makes this service distinctive

ALW serves residential and business customers with its own schedules. For studies, confirm the electric boundary and the account’s actual plan before comparing it with surrounding SCE service.

Official background: [Electric service](https://azusaca.gov/135/Electric-Services). [Separate water and electric maps](https://www.azusaca.gov/507/Map-of-Service).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Residential D and non-demand commercial G-1 import arrangements are connected to the Southern California billing, battery, and browser workflow for verified rate and rider windows.

Demand-metered and special heating/assistance plans are not enabled. There is a historical rider gap in July–December 2025. Solar settlement and cycles outside a complete supported rider window are excluded.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
