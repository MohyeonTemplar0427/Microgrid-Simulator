## Introduction and service area

Clean Energy Alliance (CEA) is a public community-choice provider serving Carlsbad, Del Mar, Escondido, Oceanside, San Marcos, Solana Beach, and Vista. SDG&E delivers the electricity and handles the combined bill.

## What makes this service distinctive

CEA is governed by its member cities and is separate from San Diego Community Power. A customer must confirm the actual generation product; a regional map does not prove which product or account vintage applies.

Official background: [CEA communities and delivery relationship](https://thecleanenergyalliance.org/faqs/). [Member-agency governance](https://thecleanenergyalliance.org/our-member-agencies/).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected residential SDG&E TOU-DR1, TOU-DR2, and EV-TOU-5 combinations with supported CEA products are implemented through the shared browser and battery workflow.

The modeled extension is limited to ordinary import accounts, supported dates, and validated local-fee conditions. Commercial, solar export, special event programs, and unverified historical periods are excluded.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
