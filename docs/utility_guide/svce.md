**Reviewed:** October 15, 2026

## Introduction and service area

Silicon Valley Clean Energy (SVCE) is a public agency purchasing electricity for participating communities in Santa Clara County. PG&E delivers the electricity. SVCE is different from the City of Santa Clara’s Silicon Valley Power and from San José Clean Energy.

## What makes this service distinctive

The region contains three similarly named but distinct service arrangements. Use the actual address and bill to identify the correct one. SVCE’s published communities include Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Cupertino, and other participating cities and unincorporated areas.

Official background: [Service communities and delivery arrangement](https://www.svcleanenergy.org/). [Public agency background](https://www.svcleanenergy.org/about/).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected commercial B-1 GreenStart and GreenPrime combinations are implemented with explicit phase and PCIA vintage, using shared billing and location-based dispatch.

Residential plans and solar settlement are not supported by this adapter. The verified date window deliberately excludes an unresolved historical spring clock-adjustment period.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
