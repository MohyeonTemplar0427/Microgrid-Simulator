## Introduction and service area

Alameda Municipal Power (AMP) is the City of Alameda’s not-for-profit electric utility. Alameda city and Alameda County are different geographic areas: an Oakland or Berkeley address is not an AMP account merely because it lies in Alameda County.

## What makes this service distinctive

AMP is a useful example of municipal service within the wider Bay Area. Its own schedules govern the bill; PG&E delivery charges and CCA generation charges should not be added to an AMP account. Residential heating type matters because the modeled baseline allowance distinguishes electric heating from other heating.

Official background: [AMP introduction](https://alamedamp.com/267/Our-Story). [Official rates](https://www.alamedamp.com/157/Alameda-Municipal-Power-Rates).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected D-1 residential and A-1, A-2, and secondary A-3 commercial import bills are implemented. The main browser supports account-confirmed import-only battery studies, with itemized bills and applicable representative electrical replay.

Onsite-generation, solar export, standby service, and unverified historical periods are outside this municipal study path. Confirm the assigned commercial schedule and a supported full billing cycle.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
