## Introduction and service area

Hetch Hetchy Power is a public electricity service operated by the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission (SFPUC). Its power system is linked to the Sierra Nevada water and hydroelectric system. It is a separate service from SFPUC’s CleanPowerSF community-choice program.

## What makes this service distinctive

An address in San Francisco does not automatically qualify for Hetch Hetchy retail service. Eligible developments and accounts need confirmation of their actual service arrangement. Do not use the CleanPowerSF-plus-PG&E bill formula for Hetch Hetchy accounts.

Official background: [SFPUC power system](https://www.sfpuc.gov/about-us/our-systems/hetch-hetchy-power-system). [Eligibility for development projects](https://www.sfpuc.gov/construction-contracts/new-developments/hetch-hetchy-power-developers).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Small-commercial C-1 and medium-commercial C-2S/C-2P, with supported Standard or confirmed Premium options, are available through shared tariff, billing, and dispatch interfaces. C-2 distinguishes secondary and primary service and requires 15-minute data.

Residential and larger special-account families are outside this adapter. Taxes and solar export settlement are excluded. Partial-month customer-charge allocation is a disclosed study approximation.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
