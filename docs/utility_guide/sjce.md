## Introduction and service area

San José Clean Energy (SJCE) is the City of San José’s community-choice electricity program. SJCE sources electricity while PG&E delivers it and maintains the local wires.

## What makes this service distinctive

A San José account should not be assigned Santa Clara’s municipal utility merely because both cities are in Silicon Valley. Electricity product selection, account enrollment, and delivery charges remain distinct. A marketed renewable share is also not an hourly emissions signal.

Official background: [About SJCE and its delivery arrangement](https://sanjosecleanenergy.org/about-sjce/).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected commercial B-1 GreenSource and TotalGreen combinations are implemented with the explicitly supported PCIA vintages and provider adjustments. The shared import-price calculation feeds billing and location-based dispatch.

The adapter does not provide general residential, assistance-program, or export-settlement coverage. Choose a vintage supported by the actual account and the executable catalog.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
