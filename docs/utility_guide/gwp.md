## Introduction and service area

Glendale Water and Power (GWP) is owned by the City of Glendale and governed locally. It supplies electricity to residential, commercial, and industrial customers in its service area.

## What makes this service distinctive

Glendale uses local and imported generation and operates within LADWP’s balancing area. That wholesale-system relationship does not make a Glendale customer an LADWP retail customer. GWP schedules, rather than nearby SCE or LADWP rates, determine the bill.

Official background: [About GWP](https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/glendale-water-and-power/about-us). [Generation and balancing-area context](https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/glendale-water-and-power/projects/proposed-grayson-power-plant-repowering-project).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected L-1-A/L-1-B, L-2-A/L-2-B, and LD-2-A ordinary secondary import arrangements use the shared Southern California billing, dispatch, and browser workflow.

Other residential options, master-meter arrangements, special riders, and solar/standby settlement remain outside the bounded adapter. Confirm historical demand requirements rather than assigning a plan from the optimized peak.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
