**Region:** Participating San Diego County jurisdictions, California  
**Role:** Community-choice generation provider with SDG&E delivery  
**Simulator status:** Selected plans implemented  
**Reviewed:** October 15, 2026

## Introduction and service area

San Diego Community Power (SDCP) purchases electricity for participating customers in Chula Vista, Encinitas, Imperial Beach, La Mesa, National City, San Diego, and unincorporated San Diego County. SDG&E remains the delivery utility.

## What makes this service distinctive

SDCP and Clean Energy Alliance are different agencies serving different member communities. Account enrollment and product must be checked separately from delivery territory. The City of San Diego and the wider county also have different relevance to local fees.

Official background: [Service and enrollment terms](https://sdcommunitypower.org/terms-and-conditions/). [Provider FAQ](https://sdcommunitypower.org/faq/).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected residential SDG&E TOU-DR1/TOU-DR2/EV-TOU-5 combinations with SDCP generation use shared billing, browser, and dispatch functionality. The account’s generation cohort and PCIA vintage are separate inputs.

The real provider serves City of San Diego accounts, but this simulator’s bounded CCA path excludes those accounts until local fee ordering is reconciled. Commercial and export arrangements are not enabled here.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
