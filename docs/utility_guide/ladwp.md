## Introduction and service area

The Los Angeles Department of Water and Power (LADWP) is a municipal utility serving Los Angeles, with a separate electric service presence in the Owens Valley. Los Angeles city and Los Angeles County are not interchangeable service territories.

## What makes this service distinctive

LADWP operates its own generation, transmission, and distribution system. Nearby SCE and municipal accounts can have different tariffs and solar rules. For a local study, use the actual service location and equipment rather than a generic countywide tariff or load shape.

Official background: [Utility introduction](https://www.ladwp.com/who-we-are). [Power system and Owens Valley service](https://www.ladwp.com/who-we-are/power-system).

## Using this provider in the simulator

Selected R-1A and R-1B residential plans and A-1A/A-1B/A-2B/A-3A commercial arrangements are included in the shared Southern California browser and dispatch workflow. A bounded residential R-1A net-import NEM cycle is also implemented.

R-1B solar settlement and R-1A net-export cycles are not covered by that NEM path. Primary/high-voltage billing support does not imply an applicable representative electrical circuit. This guide does not establish Owens Valley tariff support.

The linked implementation notes define the verified plans and date windows. Availability in a running browser also depends on its active engine version.
