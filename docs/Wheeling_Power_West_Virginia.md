# Wheeling Power West Virginia RS and SGS

The adapter implements ordinary Wheeling Power Tariff No. 21 RS residential
and SGS small general service. The joint Appalachian/Wheeling filed sheets
explicitly name both companies; this is not substitution of neighboring
utility prices. Wheeling remains a separate utility and bundled supplier,
with West Virginia jurisdiction and PJM AEP metadata.

Verified complete calendar cycles are October–November 2025 and
September–October 2026. Other 2025–2026 cycles reject. The December 2025 ENG
transition and intermediate base/ENG/RET/ENEC intersections remain unresolved.
No complete-year study is supported.

Use matching complete calendar service and bill months, a final-day bill date,
full monthly charges and consecutive Eastern-time 15-minute data. RS is ordinary
individual residential service. SGS requires independently confirmed retained
ordinary assignment and maximum electrical requirements below 10 kW; modeled
imports cannot establish this fact. Initial study peaks are RS 25 kW and SGS
9.9 kW on one single-phase secondary 120/240 V meter. Optional metering,
assistance and special-service terms are outside scope.

The original 2025 ENG factors are **0.00313 cents/kWh RS** and
**0.00245 cents/kWh SGS**. CRRC applies to Appalachian Power only. Wheeling
uses the joint filed ENEC factor without adding or subtracting an Appalachian
credit, corroborated by Wheeling's separate utility calculation workbook.
The 2026 filed stack preserves the July base step, September ENEC,
EE/DR, environmental compliance, RET and filed-zero broadband/business-ready
components. Minimum bills equal the basic service charge.

Initial locality scope requires independent confirmation of a location outside
municipalities and every local electric-tax district. Regulated electricity is
excluded from state sales tax. Local surcharges and other account programs
need separate verified modeling.

PV and paired storage require final approval effective before the service
cycle and a written utility agreement retaining ordinary import billing for
the exact installation. Batteries charge only from onsite PV. Export
settlement, NMS, COGEN/SPP and standalone storage remain unsupported.
