# Appalachian Power Virginia Standard Service

Appalachian Power Company Virginia Tariff No. 28 is available in the regional
study catalog as **R.S. Traditional Residential (015)** and **S.G.S. Small
General Service (231)**. Both require ordinary bundled Standard Service, a
single 120/240 V secondary meter, and an independently confirmed **City of
Roanoke** tax jurisdiction. City and county service are different tax scopes.
The hypothetical site near Roanoke supplies weather coordinates only. Ohio
Power, Appalachian Power West Virginia, the PJM AEP control area, any
wholesale pricing node and optional PJM regional-average carbon remain
separate identities.

## Calendar billing and eligibility

Complete matching service and bill cycles execute in **January and November
2025**, **January 2026**, and **October and November 2026**. The final service
day and bill-rendered date must agree within this scope. Other months, partial
or shifted reads, alternate suppliers or tariffs, and accounts with special
rates, tax relief or credits reject. Service-rendered riders and
bills-rendered charges intersect only inside the enabled cycles. No proration
across the March, May or September 2026 changes is inferred.

SGS 231 requires company-confirmed classification and the 12 actual
consecutive monthly metered demands immediately preceding each billed month.
An average above 25 kW requires reassignment under Tariff No. 28. The
adapter verifies the documented average and preserves history across grid-only
and DER comparisons. It does not use optimized imports to classify service.
SGS 231 has a $9.88 base monthly minimum and energy charges. RS 015 has a
$7.96 base minimum. Initial study native load and imports are bounded at
25 kW for both plans, independent of the tariff eligibility test.

Tariff Sheets 4-2 and 10-1 require a separate arrangement for small power
production. Non-export PV and PV-charged paired storage therefore require an
active, written APCo special agreement and final interconnection and metering
approval for the exact equipment. The agreement must preserve ordinary
Standard Service import billing and establish that no extra recurring
charges, minimums, settlement or other billing terms apply. Modeled
zero export alone does not establish that approval. Hypothetical examples
explicitly model a scenario where those conditions are met. Standalone
storage, NMS/NMS II, COGEN/SPP, export credits and other agreements are
outside scope. The combined DER capacity limit is 25 kW for this study.

## Components and source boundaries

The adapter itemizes base generation and distribution, fuel, transmission,
environmental, generation and capacity riders, and the Sales and Use Tax
rider. October and November 2026 apply S.B.R.R. at exactly −39.24% of
base generation and separately apply the Securitized Asset Cost rider.
The accepted May sheet and SCC financing order resolve a mislabeled subtitle
and incorrect order year printed in the later compilation. PIPP's recovery
charge changes November 1, 2026. The January 2025 SGS energy-efficiency
rate follows the filed Sheet 59 and corrected official summary. An older
illustrative sheet printed a different SGS rate.

Virginia's consumption tax uses three delivered-kWh blocks. The state,
actual SCC special regulatory levy and statutory local portions appear
separately. The January 2025 historical SCC levy has different second and
third block rates from the later levy. The City of Roanoke levies a separate
consumer utility tax. It is the greater of its two energy blocks or 12% of
the base monthly minimum. The city publishes the rates in its current
collection guidance. A full ordinance revision history and printed invoice
rounding are outside the evidence. Special residential electric-heating
relief and other exemptions require a separately verified billing model.
